# Hyphen + Apostrophe Canon Cleanup Audit

Status: Cleanup applied after human approval. This file now records the policy and the completed spelling decisions.

Purpose: identify hyphenated and apostrophe-bearing Celan forms that may need editorial cleanup now that the working policy is:

- Hyphens are grammatical scaffolding when attached to tense, honorific, number, possessive, relational, modal, passive, or other explicit grammatical markers.
- Hyphenated root-root compounds are not distinct from fused compounds unless the source explicitly defines a distinction.
- Apostrophes are preserved where attested in ritual/spiritual forms and interjections/discourse particles.
- A broad apostrophe rule should not be invented from pattern alone.

## Editorial Decision

Create `ED-0001: Hyphen and Apostrophe Cleanup`.

Decision text:

Hyphens are retained for grammatical scaffolding and explicitly attested grammatical constructions. Root-root compounds should be fused unless the source explicitly defines the hyphen as part of the form. Apostrophes are preserved where attested in ritual/spiritual vocabulary, interjections, discourse particles, and sacred/proper names. Retro-explanatory rules are not canon unless directly sourced.

## Completed Cleanup Notes

- Apostrophe style in editable Markdown/CSV files was normalized to straight apostrophe `'`.

- `PT-V2-0013` was changed to an approved cleanup record because the old `Morl-dren` / `Morldren` distinction is no longer an active canon concern.
- `Vellian` still keeps its derivation uncertainty because the source itself marks `Vel` as possibly sustenance/food or wisdom/value.
- Proposed slur rows still keep Source Ambiguity because their source status as proposed remains separate from spelling cleanup.

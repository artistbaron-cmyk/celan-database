### GR-V1-0014: Tenses and Aspects

- Source volume: Volume 1: Fundamentals
- Source section: Grammar > Tenses and Aspects
- Page number: 7
- Category: Tense/Aspect
- Canon status: Canon
- Original wording: Celan verbs do not conjugate based on tense. Instead, tenses are indicated by prefixes and suffixes.
- Examples:

  Present Tense / Default VSO / Var I dren. / “I go to the water.”
  Past Tense / Tha- (prefix) / Tha-var I dren. / “I went to the water.”
  Future Tense / Nor- (prefix) / Nor-var I dren. / “I will go to the water.”
  Continuous / -al (suffix) / Varal I dren. / “I am going to the water.”
  Perfective / -ath (suffix) / Varath I dren. / “I have gone to the water.”
  Habitual / -as (suffix) / Varas I dren. / “I usually go to the water.”

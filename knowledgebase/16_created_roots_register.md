# Created Celan Roots Register

**Status:** Current canon reference  
**As of:** 2026-09-16  
**Scope:** 86 user-approved roots created through lexical expansion  
**Authoritative data:** `data/expanded_root_database.csv` and `data/expanded_root_crosswalk.csv`

## Purpose

This register isolates the roots deliberately created during the modern Celan expansion process. It does not include inherited roots, roots promoted from older lexical evidence, grammatical morphemes, or `VAN`, the older editorially inferred market-place root.

The register answers four questions for every created root:

1. What is its stable semantic center?
2. Why was it created rather than forced from older material?
3. Which words or families have actually been approved?
4. What remains restricted or exploratory?

A root's presence here makes it active material for future root-reuse review. It does **not** automatically authorize every imaginable compound or derivative. Forms outside the approved family belong in the [Root Relationship Laboratory](17_root_relationship_laboratory.md) until separately reviewed and approved.

## Reading the family column

- The forms shown are the approved evidence associated with the root.
- A root may be independently lexicalized as a dictionary word, but not every root is automatically a visible dictionary headword.
- An approved family is evidence of productive meaning, not permission to fill an entire derivational matrix.
- Historical or phonological relationships can exist without one form being a transparent compound of another.

## Maintenance rule

When a new primitive root is approved:

1. add it to both expanded root CSVs;
2. record its approval batch, stable semantic center, approved family, and rejected alternatives;
3. add it to this register;
4. keep mechanically possible compounds outside canon;
5. move exploratory combinations into the Root Relationship Laboratory until separately approved.

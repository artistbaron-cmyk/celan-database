# Root and family update — applied locally

All 11 approved approaches are implemented. The dictionary remains at 1,496 headwords and 1,607 displayed senses. No lexical definitions, pronunciations, or source records were changed.

## RF01

Roots, prefixes and suffixes now have separate identities. -ka, -esh, -vel and other affixes no longer inherit the free root with the same letters.

## RF02

Buttons retain affix spelling. Word-specific senses are explained inline instead of opening a different root.

## RF03

Explicit prefix and suffix records participate in a separate affix inventory.

## RF04

A Built from section links recorded component words to their existing entries. Those base entries have reciprocal family links.

## RF05

Unregistered or word-specific components retain their explanations on the word. No new productive root or headword was created.

## RF06

An exact root match no longer suppresses recorded components. A historical note about Lunwek is not parsed as a derivation of Lun.

## RF07

Family lists are no longer capped in the data or exports. The page offers View all with a count and grouped connections.

## RF08

The app-facing KA explanation follows ED-0038. Standalone ka and possessive -ka remain distinct.

## RF09

Explicit reviewed family links resolve directly, including Aenvor and the reviewed -eth examples.

## RF10

Combined source headings are no longer family identities. Existing individual records and approved word-specific components are used; old source comparisons remain preserved.

## RF11

Only explicit word evidence establishes links. Formula-only source notes are retained without automatically creating words or relationships; see evidence_disposition.json for each formula.

## Validation

All-headword sense preservation, exact suffix destinations, scoped components, reciprocal component links, complete family lists, reviewed links, independent homonyms, search, earlier dictionary integration, and navigation were checked. This was runtime and rendered-markup validation, not visual browser testing.

# Review of the 109 first-example items

The [numbered review sheet](one_sentence_109_for_review.md) preserves the original 109-item order. The user reviewed items 1–50 and then 51–109. All 109 now have a visible placement in the app. The [first-batch placement record](first_50_applied.csv) and [second-batch placement record](second_59_applied.csv) name every item, its target page, and whether it was new or already present. The old app wording changed during these batches is preserved in [first-batch originals](first_50_original_placements.csv) and [second-batch originals](second_59_original_placements.csv). The earlier [112-item review](one_sentence_112_for_review.md) remains a dated snapshot.

The approved split puts the two suffix uses under **-eth** and gives standalone **Eth** its own noun meaning and approved sentence: `shalaen I eth an shalor.` — “I perceive essence in the sanctuary.” The old suffix illustrations have moved off the Eth noun page. Counted boots now use `trakorin` on every page carrying that sentence.

## Scope notes that still matter

- **#50–51 (-en)** and **#56–57 (Li-)** reuse one sentence for overlapping honorific meanings. Each sentence appears once as a general illustration. It does not prove which of the overlapping meanings is active.
- **#53 (-lin)** shows the ending inside *eshlin* (“bird”). It does not establish the full collective/diminutive range by itself.
- **#58 (-esh)** shows *thalesh* in a ritual setting. It does not prove the suffix's “deep relational ties” meaning.
- **#59 (-el)** was published with “The beloved is at the sanctuary.” The proposed “Their beloved” added a possessor that the Celan sentence did not express.
- **#72 (terra)** shows ordinary physical ground, not the sacred Terra reading. **#75 (Rethvok)** shows chaos, not the entry's separate uncertainty or conditional range.
- **#1/#3** use “guides” figuratively. **#12** shows Kav in a formed word. **#16/#38** show the grief range of Nk. These examples remain scoped illustrations rather than proof of every root meaning.

Every visible headword page now has at least one sentence or related word-building illustration. That does **not** mean every individual meaning has its own ordinary sentence. The [coverage inventory](coverage_inventory.csv) still shows **507 of 1,603 visible meanings** with fewer than two structurally possible ordinary direct sentences. **80 meanings** are on pages without an ordinary direct sentence; those pages have related illustrations. This is a placement count, not a certificate that every Celan sentence and translation has been independently verified.

## What still needs linguistic review

- **Eth:** the standalone noun is now “essence / inherent quality.” Two proposed sentences use it, but their English adds “true” or “primordial,” which the Celan does not explicitly say. A matching example still needs review.
- **-el:** `rinaen Ohmbaen-el an shalor.` — “Their beloved is at the sanctuary.” This is a new sentence built from the now-approved formed word and ordinary location pattern. Review the full sentence before publishing it.
- **Anen** and **Velian:** the app already has *anen morlak* and *velian morlak* as approved phrases. The sheet extends them into sentences. Their meanings remain simply “your” and “our,” without new formal or communal labels.
- **-esh, Nk/Nk-, -lin, Terra-/terra-, and Reth-/Rethvok-:** the candidates show only part of the wider wording on those pages. Keep them labeled as focused illustrations; do not call the full definitions proved by one sentence.
- **-en** and **Li-:** overlapping meanings remain separate in the app. A repeated sentence shows the visible form but cannot demonstrate a distinction that the sentence does not express.
- **Tharnspinners** and **Veilgliders:** the user settled these as singular species citation names, and the app now explains their final **-s**. Their draft examples still need sentence-level review before publication.

The [coverage inventory](coverage_inventory.csv) now shows **1,603 visible meanings**. **507** have fewer than two possible ordinary sentences based on current placement; **108** of these are on pages without an ordinary direct sentence. These are structural counts only; a placed sentence is not linguistically verified by this inventory.

# Sentences on the page without a meaning assignment: first review

The [original 181-meaning list](unassigned_meanings_181.csv) contained 181 meanings across 81 words. A sentence on a word's page is not automatically evidence for every meaning of that word. This review read the **16 meanings of eight words** below against all ordinary direct sentences on those pages. The corrections were applied on 2026-10-04; the tables preserve the reasons for them. These are linguistic judgments, not automated verification.

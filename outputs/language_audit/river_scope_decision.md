# Waterway meaning decision

On 2026-10-05, the creator agreed to this correction: **Dren** names water, **Dro** names ordinary physical water and also a river/waterway in geographical use, and **Drenfel** names a stream or brook.

The former visible Dren meaning **“Water / River”** (`dren-s3`, source `LX-V2-0068`) is preserved verbatim in [river_scope_retired_sense.csv](river_scope_retired_sense.csv). The current app no longer shows it as a separate Dren meaning. Dren retains its physical and symbolic water meanings.

The current app adds a distinct Dro river/waterway meaning (`dro-s3`) and assigns the existing approved example `PE-GCI1-0048` — `rinaen dro an lorzhir.` / “The river is at the border.” — to it. The existing ordinary-water Dro meaning remains.

Because Dro now has two visible meanings, its general water sentences no longer automatically count for either one. The existing `PE-SRD1-0014` — `rinaen dro an anor xarvek.` / “Water is inside the storage chest.” — is explicitly linked to the ordinary-water meaning (`dro-s2`).

Drenfel still means **stream or brook**. Its displayed word-origin explanation now reads “Dren water + Fel small,” so it no longer teaches Dren itself as the river word. The historical wording remains in the source-history files, which the app does not read.

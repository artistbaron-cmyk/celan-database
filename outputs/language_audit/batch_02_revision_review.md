A and B refer to the first and second proposed sentences under each word.

## Decisions still needed

- Are *Tharnspinners* and *Veilgliders* singular species names in Celan despite their English-looking `-s`, or do these sentences need plural marking/translation? The app currently gives each a singular noun definition but does not settle this number question.
- Does the Zhenlor story intend a literal guide of citizens, or would another scene better reflect the legend? The revised sentence is grammatical either way, but the lore claim is not established by the word meanings.

The user-proposed lines and this review remain proposals. They do not count toward the app's two-example goal until a reviewed version is approved and placed under the correct meaning.

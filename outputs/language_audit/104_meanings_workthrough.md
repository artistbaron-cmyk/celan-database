## Decisions still genuinely about language scope

Most of the work is example review, citation repair, or duplicate-label cleanup. The one scope question that remains open on current evidence is whether **Dren itself retains a river sense**, or whether a river should be expressed with **Dro** or another dedicated term. The creator's proposed batch expresses a preference for routing river to Dro, but Dro's current definition has not yet been reconciled with that. Other currently visible but unillustrated senses—such as **Vok “betray,” Pral “make,” Kor “handspan,” and Thael as an adjective**—are preserved according to prior decisions while better examples are sought. No meaning should be removed just because this sentence count did not find an illustration.

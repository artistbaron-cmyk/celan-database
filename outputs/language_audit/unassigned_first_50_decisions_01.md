Received after the [first-50 review sheet](unassigned_first_50_for_review.md). The decision and reasoning below preserve the creator's review and later correction; the application notes record what was changed in the app. No decision for items 11–50 has been supplied yet.

**Later correction for Ilin, now applied:** The creator rejected the proposed Imen contrast on Ilin's dictionary page. The inclusion distinction is a grammar rule; the dictionary sentence's job is to show **Ilin** being used. The two Ilin entries repeated the same inclusive meaning, so they were merged into one **We / us (inclusive)** meaning. `velaen Ilin morlak.` is linked to it. The former `ilin-s2` row is preserved in [retired senses](unassigned_first_50_retired_senses.csv). No Imen dialogue was added. The earlier hold notes below remain as review history and are superseded by this correction.

## Ilin — items 1–2

**1. We / Us [Inclusive]: Hold / Refine Example.** `velaen Ilin morlak` demonstrates Ilin as a first-person plural subject, but the standalone sentence does not show whether the listener is included. The creator suggested keeping it as a general/default example or adding `servok` to make the shared context explicit. **Applied:** no sense-specific link or sentence rewrite. The existing sentence remains visible at word level.

**2. Inclusive / communal default we: Hold / Refine Example.** The creator confirms **Ilin** includes speaker and listener; **Imen** is exclusive and excludes the listener. The existing bread sentence lacks the conversational contrast. The creator suggested a note or secondary dialogue example contrasting Ilin and Imen; the supplied illustration was `Arth Imen an kaleth. Var Ya.` — “We protect the fortress; you must go.” **Applied:** no sense-specific link or new dialogue. This illustration has not been added or separately checked for sentence accuracy.

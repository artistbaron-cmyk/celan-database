# Example review batch 01 — people and body parts

**Status: approved by the user and applied on 2026-10-01.** All 12 new sentences below are in the Dictionary App and linked to their specific meanings. The two placements of “Kadfel ohmbaen” now appear as a phrase. The eight placements of the two obsolete Volume 1 sentences have been removed from student-facing app data and preserved in [batch_01_retired_examples.csv](batch_01_retired_examples.csv). This batch supplies two reviewed sentence examples for each of the nine listed meanings, counting the retained sentence where shown.

| Meaning | Previous direct sentence | Review finding | Approved first / additional sentence | Approved second sentence | Decision applied |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Kadfel**, noun, boy | `Kadfel ohmbaen` — “Beloved boy.” | A phrase, not a sentence; also shown under Ohmbaen. | `drenaen kadfel dren.` — “The boy drinks water.” | `var kadfel an teremil.` — “The boy goes home.” | Approve both new sentences and reclassify the old record as a phrase in both placements. |
| **Azfel**, noun, girl | `Azfel kal dren morl` — “The girl brings strength to the flowing mountain.” | The Celan does not express the English action and does not follow current verb-first sentence order. The same record appears under Kal, Dren, and Morl. | `shalaen azfel eshlin.` — “The girl sees a bird.” | `drenaen azfel dren.` — “The girl drinks water.” | Approve both new sentences and remove the obsolete record from all four student-facing placements. |
| **Theefel**, noun, neutral child | `Theefel pralmor an shal` — “The neutral child creates with the light.” | *Pralmor* means “detailed carving,” not “creates”; the Celan does not support this translation. The same record appears under Pralmor, An, and Shal. | `shalaen theefel eshlin.` — “The neutral child sees a bird.” | `var theefel an teremil.` — “The neutral child goes home.” | Approve both new sentences and remove the obsolete record from all four student-facing placements. |
| **Kadon**, noun, masculine woman | `var kadon an teremil.` — “The masculine woman goes home.” | Retain; verb, subject, and destination fit the current pattern. | Keep the current sentence. | `drenaen kadon dren.` — “The masculine woman drinks water.” | Approve the additional sentence. |
| **Aenvor**, noun, nose | `drenselaen I aenvor-ian.` — “I wash my nose.” | Retain; the body-part possession pattern is approved. | Keep the current sentence. | `keshenaen zhaelral aenvor-ian.` — “The healer examines my nose.” | Approve the additional sentence. |
| **Sorl**, noun, neck | `rinaen aiv an sorl-ian.` — “There is pain in my neck.” | Retain; the same location and possession pattern is in approved usage. | Keep the current sentence. | `keshenaen zhaelral sorl-ian.` — “The healer examines my neck.” | Approve the additional sentence. |
| **Kalvar**, noun, arm | `drenselaen I kalvar-ian.` — “I wash my arm.” | Retain; the body-part possession pattern is approved. | Keep the current sentence. | `rinaen aiv an kalvar-ian.` — “There is pain in my arm.” | Approve the additional sentence. |
| **Sharvar**, noun, leg | `keshenaen zhaelral sharvar-ian.` — “The healer examines my leg.” | Retain; the verb, subject, object, and possession fit the current pattern. | Keep the current sentence. | `drenselaen I sharvar-ian.` — “I wash my leg.” | Approve the additional sentence. |
| **Morlka**, noun, foot | `drenselaen I morlka-ian.` — “I wash my foot.” | Retain; the body-part possession pattern is approved. | Keep the current sentence. | `rinaen aiv an morlka-ian.` — “There is pain in my foot.” | Approve the additional sentence. |

**Language basis checked:** the app meanings of *drenaen* (drink), *var* (go), *shalaen* (see), *eshlin* (bird), *teremil* (home), *drenselaen* (wash), *keshenaen* (clinical examination), *zhaelral* (healer), *aiv* (pain), and the approved post-nominal *-ian* possession pattern. This is a targeted review of these examples; it is not approval of every app sentence using those words.

# Nevu / arin: distance candidate review

Status: proposals only. No dictionary or grammar integration.
Method: manual composition, meaning, reference-point, and ambiguity review against current app senses and grammar. Written cadence assessment only; no audio or independent comprehension testing.

## Candidate definitions

- **nevu**, proposed adjective, /neh-voo/: spatially near a stated or contextually understood reference point.
- **arin**, proposed adjective, /ah-reen/: spatially far from a stated or contextually understood reference point.

Arin is already named as a far-reaching component in Zharin's visible usage note. Making it an independent spatial adjective requires approval; the kinship meaning remains intact. Nevu is a proposed new primitive, with no asserted historical derivation. Both are universal candidates.

Root reuse: Esh covers sky/air and related distance imagery but also means above; it does not cleanly provide general distance endpoints. Tla- is intimate connection, not physical proximity. Den identifies a participant's sphere, not the amount of separation. Felath is almost. An supplies spatial location. These should retain their established distinctions. Arin is the strongest available latent component for far; an independent near primitive avoids overloading intimacy, smallness, or deixis.

## Twelve cases

All Celan below using nevu or independent arin is proposed usage. The adjective + thal + reference construction is part of the proposed package, supported by thal's existing from meaning but not presented as an already attested distance pattern.

| Case | Candidate / scenario | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **rinaen evan nevu.** — The market is nearby. | Coherent with an understood reference, usually the current location in a directions conversation. The context must supply that reference. |
| 2 | **rinaen evan arin.** — The market is far away. | Coherent under the same condition. No extra adverbial headword needed. |
| 3 | **rinaen evan nevu thal deni.** — The market is near here. | Strong candidate for an explicit reference construction. Deni supplies the speaker's sphere and thal the from relation. |
| 4 | **rinaen evan arin thal denya.** — The market is far from there/by you. | Can work, but denya is specifically the listener's sphere. It cannot silently mean any previously mentioned distant location. |
| 5 | **rinaen evan nevu thal theren.** — The market is near the village. | Strong candidate. A named location prevents confusion about which person is the reference. |
| 6 | **rinaen evan nevu ther theren.** — The market is nearer than the village. | Fits established adjective comparison. Both destinations must be compared from the SAME reference point. It does not mean the market is near the village. |
| 7 | **rinaen evan arin ther theren.** — The market is farther than the village. | Same successful structure and same shared-reference requirement. |
| 8 | **rinaen evan nevu thaal.** — The market is nearest. | Fits established superlative structure. Requires a contextually identified set of destinations and shared reference point. |
| 9 | **rinaen evan arin thaal.** — The market is farthest. | Same conditions. No new comparative or superlative suffix needed. |
| 10 | **ra rinaen evan arin thal deni?** | Under current ordinary question grammar: Is the market far from here? It does NOT explicitly request the distance. |
| 11 | **ra rinaen evan arin thal deni sha?** — intended: How far is the market from here? | Requires a NEW narrow degree-question convention. Sha currently asks manner/method/means. Under the proposed convention, arin + closing sha asks degree of spatial separation without asserting that the destination is far. Answers such as very near and a stated distance must both be allowed. This meaning cannot be claimed from current grammar alone. |
| 12 | Near across a river, but a long journey by road; near versus touching; not-near versus far. | Keep spatial separation separate from route length and travel time. Neither adjective implies contact, reachability, safety, or journey duration. Negating near need not assert far: a middle distance is possible. |

## Sound and collision review

Neither candidate is a current app headword. Nevu has no exact occurrence in the checked app CSV files or either root inventory. Arin occurs in Zharin's derivation note. Existing **Nevar** (nine) and **Varin** (know) are useful listening checks: nevu/Nevar differ in ending and arin/Varin differ in initial consonant. Similar spelling alone does not establish confusion. The two proposed distance words have clearly different beginnings and vowel sequences. Written phrases nevu ther theren and arin ther theren remain articulable on desk review; actual listening validation remains outstanding.

## Recommendation

The pair passes the written composition review as a useful proposal. Nearer/farther and nearest/farthest use existing comparison grammar. Approve explicit reference-point usage together with the adjective meanings if the forms are selected.

For how far, the smallest proposed addition is a limited distance-degree question: **ra + rinaen + place + arin + optional thal reference + sha?** Do not extend sha to every degree question or derive a general how-many/how-much rule from this one construction. The distance question remains a proposed grammar decision, not a test-proven existing meaning.

Do not add emotional closeness, temporal nearness, kinship meanings for nevu, or a full derivative family automatically.

# Celan Proposal: Body, Internal Anatomy, and Ordinary Bodily Functions — Batch 1

**Status:** Approved and integrated into canon on 2026-09-15 (ED-0033)
**Scope:** Universal Celan  
**Proposed package:** 44 headwords  
**Origin nation:** None; all proposed forms are universal  
**Design register:** Ordinary speech, caregiving, field medicine, and clinical use

## Purpose

Celan can already name the head, face, neck, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, arms, hands, legs, feet, heart, blood, and bone. It also has strong language for pain, wounds, illness, fatigue, hunger, thirst, bleeding, swallowing, sleep, unconsciousness, treatment, and recovery.

What it cannot yet do is let someone point to most of the body, name the major internal organs, or speak plainly about common involuntary and eliminative functions.

This is not an Earth-derived technical checklist. Ohnoshans inhabit vulnerable bodies, treat injuries, care for children and elders, butcher animals, encounter unfamiliar beings, and survive conditions in which recognizing where a body hurts or what it is doing can matter immediately. Those needs justify direct words.

The proposal deliberately does **not** force ordinary anatomy into civil-engineering or elemental metaphor. Such metaphors may exist in healing traditions, jokes, idioms, and technical theory, but a person should not have to call the brain a “control interface” or the lungs an “air chamber” merely to identify them.

## Admission logic

- Short, direct forms are preferred for frequently named body parts and organs.
- Existing roots are reused only where they produce an obvious ordinary word: hand + joint for *wrist*, foot + finger/digit for *toe*, swallow + place for *throat*, blood + passage for *blood vessel*, and blood + cycle for *menstruation*.
- No new word is proposed for body concepts already covered by `Lorinor`, `Rema`, `Sorl`, `Lorinshal`, `Aenor`, `Aenvor`, `Kelvor`, `Kalvar`, `Korin`, `Sharvar`, `Morlka`, `Thar`, `Ndem`, or `Osar`.
- A new root may earn admission through semantic connection, a credible historical path, phonological belonging, or a necessary distinction. It need not satisfy all four, and no sound pattern functions as an authenticity marker.
- The 44 entries are a semantic result, not a target. Thumb, heel, tendon, ligament, artery, vein, pancreas, spleen, gallbladder, and detailed reproductive anatomy remain for later review rather than being added to inflate this batch.

## Boundaries

- `Doran` is the whole trunk. `Breka`, `Vesh`, and `Davel` name regions of it.
- `Breka` is anatomical chest, not `Xarvek`, a storage chest.
- `Vesh` is the body part, not `Vethvar`, the positional direction behind.
- `Xarmetha` means living protective skin. `Rask` remains removed animal skin or raw hide. Neither word silently expands into the other.
- `Metha` is flesh or soft tissue in ordinary description. It is not every specialized tissue type.
- `Gora` is anatomical. It does not automatically mean a hinge, coupling, civic junction, or meeting place.
- `Korkalvar` and `Korsharvar` use `Kor` in its source/core sense, not its handspan measurement sense.
- `Kalvargora`, `Koringora`, `Sharvargora`, and `Morlkagora` form a bodily joint series. They do not make every arm, hand, leg, or foot reference a joint.
- `Pir` includes the thumb in unmarked counting. A later direct word for thumb should be admitted only if repeated contrastive use proves that Celan needs one.
- `Numor` is the throat region involved in swallowing. `Sorl` remains the externally identifiable neck.

## Why these are direct nouns

These organs are frequent enough in injury, illness, food preparation, animal care, and healing to deserve stable names. Their definitions describe what speakers identify; they do not claim that every Ohnoshan medical tradition shares a modern Earth taxonomy or theory.

The compounds in this section follow bodily functions that are stable enough to identify the structure without pretending to provide a complete medical theory. `Aentash` is the breath organ; `Emiltash` is the first food-receiving organ; `Emilshan` is the food passage beyond it; and `Urenvek` is the urine-holding organ. `Ndemshan` does not imply a manufactured pipe: `Shan` already means passage or transfer and the compound is restricted to a living blood passage.

## Register and meaning boundaries

- All fifteen forms are neutral everyday vocabulary. They may be used in a clinic, home, school, work crew, or travel party without sounding ceremonial or obscene.
- `Khos`/`Khosaen` and `Zer`/`Zeraen` are compact reflex families rather than long literal compounds. Their sound symbolism offers a plausible spoken origin, but the forms do not imitate an actual cough or sneeze so narrowly that speakers could not say them quietly.
- `Suv` is bodily perspiration, not water, dew, condensation, or any moisture on a surface.
- `Nar` is nausea, while `Naraen` is the actual expulsion of stomach contents. A person may experience `Nar` without `Naraen`.
- `Uren` and `Fek` are bodily materials. Their verbs name release, not the location, waste-disposal system, insult, or social judgment.
- `Ndemwek` names menstruation without treating it as injury, impurity, illness, or ordinary wound bleeding. `Ndemaen` remains the general verb *to bleed*.
- `Kakesrinaen` is intransitive: the subject faints. Causing someone to lose consciousness is not authorized as the same verb.

# Root-reuse and primitive review

## Second-pass result

The first draft used 31 new roots or unanalyzable stems. This pass reduces that number to **20 proposed primitive roots**. The reduction comes from real semantic relationships, not a ban on new vocabulary:

- one `Gora` joint family now names shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, and ankle through established limb words;
- one `Metha` soft-tissue family now names flesh, living skin, and muscle;
- one `Tash` organ family now supports lung and stomach;
- existing `Shan` supports intestine and blood vessel;
- new `Uren` supports urine, urination, and bladder;
- `Khos`, `Zer`, `Suv`, `Nar`, and `Fek` each support a small ordinary bodily family.

Twenty is not a quota or an automatic acceptable total. Each root still has to earn admission independently.

## Existing material successfully reused

| Existing form | Proposed reuse | Why the relationship is genuine |
|---|---|---|
| `Kor` | `Korkalvar`, `Korsharvar` | Shoulder and hip are the source or upper joining regions of the established arm and leg. |
| `Kalvar`, `Korin`, `Sharvar`, `Morlka` | joint and digit compounds | These are already the ordinary arm, hand, leg, and foot words; the new terms refine rather than replace them. |
| `Kal` | `Kalmetha` | Muscle is force-producing soft bodily tissue; the compound does not mean strength metaphorically. |
| `Xar` | `Xarmetha` | Living skin is bodily tissue functioning as an outer protective covering. |
| `Aen` | `Aentash` | The lung is the organ directly involved in bodily breathing; `Aen` already includes breath. |
| `Emil` | `Emiltash`, `Emilshan` | Food reception and onward digestive passage distinguish stomach and intestine in ordinary bodily talk. |
| `Num` | `Numor` | `Num` already names swallowing; `-or` locates the bodily passage where it occurs. |
| `Ndem` | `Ndemshan`, `Ndemwek` | Blood is genuinely definitional for a blood vessel and menstrual bleeding. |
| `Shan` | `Emilshan`, `Ndemshan` | Passage or transfer is physical and established beyond manufactured conduits. |
| `Wek` | `Ndemwek` | Recurrence distinguishes menstruation from an injury or isolated bleeding event. |
| `Vek` | `Urenvek` | The bladder is the bodily structure that contains urine before release; the compound is lexically restricted to that organ. |
| `Kakesrin` | `Kakesrinaen` | Existing unconsciousness supports the intransitive event of suddenly entering that state. |

## Historical honesty

No candidate in this proposal is declared to come from Arvan, Trerran, a particular nation, or an unnamed pre-Day-One language. Those relationships are still open design questions. Frequently used body vocabulary would plausibly have stabilized early in contact Celan, but plausibility is not an etymology.

`Khos` and `Zer` have a possible sound-symbolic formation path. That is a formation hypothesis, not a claim of direct borrowing from an Earth language. The other irreducible roots are admitted through necessary distinction and sound fit unless a later historical layer gives them stronger provenance.

## Phonological and influence review

The proposal uses the full Celan sound space as a field rather than treating any one onset or cluster as proof of authenticity.

- Compact forms such as `Pir`, `Zek`, `Tash`, `Lesh`, `Khos`, `Zer`, `Suv`, `Nar`, and `Fek` support fast bodily reference and urgent speech.
- Open or released forms such as `Breka`, `Gora`, `Lera`, and `Khelu` balance consonantal attack with the five-vowel framework.
- `Doran`, `Davel`, `Lumar`, and `Kedir` use longer rhythmic contours where a one-syllable form would create crowding or false similarity.
- Compounds alternate familiar semantic pieces rather than decorating new words with `Nd-`, `Mb-`, `Nk-`, `Tl-`, apostrophes, or extra clusters. Those resources remain fully available when their meaning or history supports them.
- No real-world language name belongs in a dictionary derivation. Creator-facing influence should remain specific and generous, but resemblance alone is not evidence of descent or borrowing.

The roots remain candidates. Phonological fit supports a semantically justified word; it cannot rescue an unnecessary one.

# Collision and confusion audit

The candidate forms were checked against the complete source lexicon and approved expansion lexicon.

- No proposed headword is an exact collision with an existing headword.
- `Breka` replaces an earlier draft form `Kavel`, which was rejected because `Kavel-` is already active in memory-loss vocabulary.
- `Xarmetha` replaces draft `Savel`; the new form joins skin to the proposed bodily-tissue family while remaining distinct from `Rask` animal skin/raw hide.
- `Kalmetha` replaces draft `Mesk` and abandoned `Kalar`; it connects muscle to bodily tissue without colliding with `Kalaru` physical magic.
- `Korkalvar`, `Kalvargora`, `Korsharvar`, `Sharvargora`, and `Morlkagora` replace five isolated draft roots with one joint system grounded in existing body vocabulary.
- `Aentash`, `Emiltash`, `Emilshan`, and `Urenvek` replace four isolated organ forms with bounded functional compounds.
- `Vesh` resembles but does not reuse `Veth-`; its meaning must not be analyzed as the directional or shadow root.
- `Nar` appears as a letter sequence inside several number and unrelated compound forms, but it has no exact headword collision. Its short everyday form should still receive spoken-cadence testing before approval.
- `Lesh` appears inside `Drenvelesh` morning dew but has no independent competing headword.
- `Ndemshan`, `Ndemwek`, `Koringora`, `Morlkapir`, `Numor`, and the new family compounds are transparently formed and therefore intentionally contain existing or proposed roots.

# What this batch intentionally does not settle

The following should receive a second anatomy pass only after these basics are tested in scenes:

- thumb and heel as independently necessary everyday headwords;
- tendon, ligament, cartilage, marrow, fat, and connective tissue;
- artery and vein, including whether Ohnoshan healers classify blood vessels by direction of flow, pressure, depth, color, or another observable property;
- pancreas, spleen, gallbladder, and finer digestive anatomy;
- reproductive organs and genital anatomy;
- saliva, mucus, tears, phlegm, and other bodily fluids;
- hiccup, belch, flatulence, bowel urgency, constipation, diarrhea, and other ordinary functions;
- smile, grin, bite, and deliberate tooth-baring, to be reviewed together with `Zek` rather than attached automatically to the tooth root;
- species-specific anatomy for nonhuman peoples and SuperBeings.

This deferral is not a claim that the concepts are unimportant. It prevents one proposal from smuggling in a modern anatomical textbook and gives Ohnoshan healing practice room to determine where finer distinctions belong.

# Approval boundary

Approval of this proposal would authorize only the 44 listed headwords, pronunciations, meanings, categories, derivations, and stated boundaries, including the 20 explicitly identified primitive roots.

It would **not** authorize:

- any deferred anatomy or bodily-function term;
- automatic adjective, agent, diminutive, plural, slang, euphemistic, or causative derivatives;
- redefinition of `Rask`, `Sorl`, `Vethvar`, `Xarvek`, `Ndemaen`, or `Kakesrin`;
- any claim that the functional glosses constitute the whole of Ohnoshan medical theory;
- integration into canonical CSVs or the dictionary application before explicit approval.

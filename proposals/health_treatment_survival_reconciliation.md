# Health, Treatment, and Survival — Reconciliation

**Status:** Pre-candidate audit; not approved vocabulary  
**Scope:** Universal Celan vocabulary  
**Target layer:** Clinical recognition, treatment, medicine, exposure injury, emergency response, evacuation, quarantine, recovery, and survival procedure  
**Audit baseline:** 1,069 app headwords, 1,147 displayed senses, 517 approved expansion rows, and 145 stored euphonic or alias forms

## Purpose

This document reconciles the proposed health, treatment, and survival category against the entire assembled Canon Dictionary before any new forms are proposed. Domestic and Sensory Life 1 already established much of the ordinary bodily foundation. The next expansion should build a medical and emergency-response layer without duplicating that vocabulary or silently broadening its definitions.

No headword in this document is proposed for approval. The surviving gaps are divided into two candidate-development passes so that root reuse, pronunciation, cadence, examples, and collision review can be completed before canon approval is requested.

## Design commitments for the category

1. Existing bodily-state, pain, illness, injury, food, water, protection, movement, and safety vocabulary remains primary.
2. Treatment performed by another person and recovery undergone by the patient remain distinct actions.
3. Rescue and evacuation remain distinct: rescue centers an endangered being, while evacuation centers organized removal from a dangerous place.
4. Bodily dehydration remains distinct from thirst, ordinary dryness, and the deliberate dehydration of food or materials.
5. Unconsciousness remains distinct from sleep, rest, dreaming, sedation, confusion, and ordinary lack of attention.
6. Infection remains distinct from poison, rot, injury, and illness in general.
7. Generic medicine must be broader than the established herbal remedy.
8. A medicinal dose may deserve a headword, but a dosage schedule should first be tested as a transparent construction using dose, time, repetition, and sequence language.
9. These meanings are universal overall. National origin and national usage should remain blank unless later review identifies a genuinely nation-specific use.
10. All eventual entries must use existing dictionary categories. No new grammatical category is proposed.
11. The unsmoothed construction should remain the primary headword. Any approved smoother form would be an optional searchable variant.
12. Approval of a future batch would authorize only its explicitly listed entries and senses, not automatic derivative families.

## Reconciliation of requested meanings

| Requested meaning | Finding | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Genuine gap | No lexical verb or medical diagnosis noun exists. `Mekral` mentions diagnosing systems only inside its English definition. |
| Treatment or medical care | Genuine gap | Must mean deliberate intervention intended to relieve or correct illness or injury, not merely empathy, protection, or ordinary caretaking. |
| Medicine, generic | Partial coverage | `Fennilphel` covers herbal remedies only. The generic term must include substances, preparations, magic, and other therapeutic interventions where appropriate. |
| Dose | Genuine gap | `Shen` supplies measurement, but no word means one measured amount of medicine given at a time. |
| Dosage | Likely transparent construction | First test a dose word with existing time, repetition, sequence, and instruction language before admitting a separate regimen headword. |
| Fever | Genuine gap | Must name illness-associated elevation of bodily heat, not environmental heat, a heatwave, anger, or surface burning pain. |
| Infection | Genuine gap | Must name harmful biological invasion or growth in a body or wound. It must not collapse into poison, rot, generic illness, or every contagious condition. |
| Frostbite | Genuine gap | Must name tissue injury caused by severe cold or freezing, not coldness, shivering, or ordinary pain alone. |
| Bodily dehydration | Genuine gap after reconciliation | `Drorav` is thirst, `Kadreneth` is dry or waterless, and `Vindrenaen` is deliberate material or food drying. None names a dangerous bodily deficit of water. |
| Unconsciousness | Genuine gap | Must name involuntary absence of wakeful awareness and responsiveness. Sleep, sedation, confusion, dreaming, and death remain separate. |
| Rescue | Genuine gap | Existing carrying, movement, protection, safety, and danger vocabulary lacks the defining outcome of removing a being from immediate danger or confinement. |
| Evacuation | Genuine gap | Must name organized removal of people or living beings from a dangerous place. It is not synonymous with retreat, boarding, transport, or rescue. |
| Quarantine | Genuine gap | Must name health-directed separation intended to prevent possible transmission. Ordinary enclosure, privacy, imprisonment, and military isolation remain separate. |
| Physical recovery | Partial coverage | `Zhael` supplies renewal and `Zhaelral` names a healer, but no displayed verb or state names a body returning toward health or function. |
| Survival | Genuine gap | Must mean continued life or function despite danger, deprivation, or damaging conditions, not victory, endurance alone, or mere continued existence. |
| Survival procedure or safety protocol | Partial coverage | Celan can express order, instruction, safety, danger, and sequence, but lacks a general lexicalized procedure established to prevent harm or guide emergency action. |

## Meanings excluded as duplicates

The next health batch should not add new words whose primary meanings are any of the following:

- sickness or illness in general;
- sick or ill;
- wound or injured bodily site;
- pain in general;
- ache, sting, burning pain, itch, or shiver;
- bodily fatigue;
- hunger or thirst;
- swallowing, drinking, feeding, washing, or resting;
- poison or toxin;
- herbal remedy;
- restorative healer;
- ordinary safety, danger, hazard avoidance, carrying, transport, or retreat.

An eventual candidate may use these established forms in its derivation, but it must preserve their approved meanings and contribute a independently lexicalized distinction.

## Preliminary root-reuse direction

This section identifies active semantic material to test. It does not authorize any compound or derivative.

- **Physical recovery:** `Zhael` renewal is the strongest existing center. `Zhaelral` already proves a restorative bodily application, while `Zhaelpralaen` must remain object or system repair. Any bodily verb needs an explicit boundary and approval.
- **Illness:** `Mav` is the established generic illness root and should constrain fever, infection, symptoms, diagnosis, and medicine where the construction remains honest.
- **Injury and pain:** `Aiv` and `Aivkor` distinguish physical pain from a wounded bodily site. They are promising material for frostbite and wound-care constructions.
- **Heat and cold:** `Krez` supplies heat and `Vel` supplies cold endurance or stillness. Neither alone supplies a medical condition.
- **Water deficit:** `Dro` supplies basic physical water, `Dren` broader liquid and flow, and source-established `Ka-dren` supplies waterlessness in `Kadreneth`. Any dehydration form must avoid confusion with the independent `Kadren` masculine-essence root and with approved `Vindrenaen` food or material drying.
- **Recognition and diagnosis:** `Kes` supplies noticing, `Shen` measurement, `Lian` truth or correctness, `Zhirkor` cause, and `Shenral` systematic investigation. These may support symptoms or assessment, but none currently means examination or determination of a condition. Diagnosis remains a possible primitive-root pressure point if compounds distort the act.
- **Dose:** `Shen` measurement is the leading root. Generic medicine must be settled before a compound for medicinal dose can be evaluated honestly.
- **Treatment:** `Zhael` renewal, `Pral` skilled deliberate work, `Tal` giving or transfer, and `Ral` entrusted care are relevant. Treatment must not be reverse-engineered as generic making, giving, or empathy.
- **Consciousness:** `Kes` awareness, `Sel` mental clarity, `Veth` sleep or hiddenness, `Neth` rest, and `Rin` inner essence are relevant but incomplete. A candidate must not define unconsciousness as ordinary sleep or spiritual absence.
- **Rescue and evacuation:** `Vek` carrying, `Var` movement, `Arth` protection, `Vaar` safety, `Rathlor` danger, `An` inward, `Thal` outward, and `Shara` route or organized course are relevant. The lexical form must encode the safety-directed purpose rather than merely movement.
- **Quarantine:** `Kavok` separation, `Xar` enclosure, `Bel` controlled restriction, and eventual infection or transmission vocabulary are relevant. Military siege, imprisonment, privacy, and ordinary isolation are insufficient models.
- **Procedure:** `Shara` ordered course, `Belwek` recurring ordered process, `Kel` stated instruction, and `Vaar` safety are relevant. A procedure word should be useful beyond one emergency while remaining narrower than any path, policy, or recipe.

### Primitive-root pressure points

Two meanings currently place the greatest pressure on the inherited and approved inventory:

1. **Infection and transmission.** `Mav` gives illness, but not harmful biological invasion, colonization, or transmission. `Ruvan` gives uncontrolled biological decay, which is not the same process. `Bel` gives connection, which would misleadingly make every linked illness an infection. A primitive may be justified if scene testing confirms a productive family spanning infection, infected state, and transmission without conflating them.
2. **Clinical examination and diagnosis.** `Kes`, `Shen`, `Lian`, and `Zhirkor` supply noticing, measurement, correctness, and cause, but no active root clearly means examining evidence to identify a condition. A reusable assessment root may serve both bodily and mechanical diagnosis, but only if it does not duplicate ordinary noticing, research, or general understanding.

No primitive should be proposed until the full collision and cadence review demonstrates that these existing roots cannot carry the intended meanings cleanly.

## Meaning boundaries and contrast tests

| Contrast | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| illness / infection | `Mav` covers disturbed bodily health regardless of cause. Infection requires harmful biological presence or activity and must not become the default explanation for every illness. |
| infection / poison | Infection involves living or biologically active invasion or growth. `Mavmor` is a harmful substance acting through exposure and does not reproduce as an infection does. |
| infection / rot | `Ruvan` is uncontrolled biological decay of once-living material or a social metaphor. Infection can occur in living tissue without that tissue rotting. |
| symptom / diagnosis | A symptom or sign is evidence noticed or experienced. A diagnosis is a reasoned identification of the condition producing or explaining evidence. |
| treatment / recovery | Treatment is an intervention performed or applied. Recovery is improvement occurring in the injured or ill being, whether aided or unaided. |
| medicine / herbal remedy | Generic medicine includes `Fennilphel` but is not limited to plant-based preparations. |
| dose / dosage schedule | A dose is one measured amount administered at one time. A schedule or regimen orders repeated doses across time. |
| fever / heat | Fever is an illness-associated bodily state. `Krez`, `Rek`, and `Krezrek` retain heat, peak intensity, and heatwave meanings. |
| frostbite / cold | Frostbite requires bodily tissue injury. Being cold, shivering, or enduring winter does not imply frostbite. |
| dehydration / thirst | `Drorav` is the felt bodily pull toward water. Dehydration is an unsafe physiological deficit and may exist before, during, or without consciously reported thirst. |
| dehydration / deliberate drying | `Vindrenaen` is a controlled action applied to food or material. Bodily dehydration is normally an unwanted condition, not a preservation technique. |
| unconsciousness / sleep | Sleep is an ordinary reversible state with its own rhythm and vocabulary. Unconsciousness is involuntary lack of wakeful awareness or responsiveness. |
| unconsciousness / death | An unconscious being remains bodily alive. `Duma` remains the irreversible end of bodily life. |
| rescue / treatment | Rescue removes someone from immediate danger or confinement. Treatment addresses illness or injury and may occur before, during, or after rescue. |
| rescue / evacuation | Rescue centers one or more endangered beings and may be improvised. Evacuation is organized removal from a dangerous place and may occur before anyone is injured or trapped. |
| evacuation / retreat | Evacuation removes people from a place of danger. `Vethvaraen` is purposeful withdrawal by the moving party, especially from danger or combat, and does not imply organized civilian removal. |
| quarantine / imprisonment | Quarantine is health-directed and preventive, normally tied to suspected transmission. Imprisonment restrains under punitive, coercive, or security authority. |
| survival / victory | Survival means continuing to live or function despite danger. It does not imply defeating an opponent, succeeding at the mission, or escaping without injury. |
| safety protocol / policy | A safety protocol is an established sequence of actions for preventing or responding to harm. `Lorshara` policy guides recurring institutional decisions but need not specify executable steps. |

## Scene tests for candidate development

These are English capability tests, not proposed canonical Celan translations.

| Scene | Existing support | Current dead ends |

## Validation and preservation findings

- The lexicon audit reports 1,069 app headwords, 1,147 displayed senses, 1,256 assembled underlying records, 517 approved expansion rows, 512 expansion headwords in the app, 145 stored variants, and zero missing expansion pronunciations.
- The assembled expansion validator confirms that displayed senses are preserved. It currently reports one informational warning because approved `Lun` also exists in the source lexicon; this is the known additive lake/Moon polysemy rather than a lost sense.
- The dictionary search test currently contains one stale expected-example list. `Dresh` now retrieves the later approved contrastive example `PE-SCP1-0016` in addition to its original two material examples. The failure indicates an outdated test fixture, not missing or corrupted dictionary data.
- No dictionary, expansion, example, root, or generated app data was changed during this reconciliation.

## Approval boundary

Approval of this reconciliation would approve only the category division and semantic boundaries. It would not authorize:

- any new Celan headword, sense, pronunciation, example, or variant;
- either candidate pool as a fixed batch;
- any new primitive root;
- any automatic derivative family;
- replacement or broadening of an existing definition;
- reinterpretation of `Vindrenaen` as bodily dehydration;
- reinterpretation of sleep as unconsciousness;
- reinterpretation of poison or rot as infection;
- a national origin for universal medical or survival vocabulary;
- or canonical integration before a separate, explicit candidate approval.

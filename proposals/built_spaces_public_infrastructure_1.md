# Built Spaces and Public Infrastructure — Proposal 1

**Status:** Approved for canonical integration on 2026-09-12  
**Scope:** Universal Celan vocabulary  
**Target layer:** Buildings, civic spaces, structural elements, and public infrastructure  
**Integration state:** Nothing in this document has been added to the canonical dictionary.

## Design commitments for this batch

1. Existing Celan meanings remain primary. A new word is proposed only where the current dictionary does not already provide the ordinary built-space sense.
2. These words are universal rather than nationally owned. Their national origin should therefore remain blank if approved.
3. Every written letter contributes to the spoken form. Celan's established digraphs may represent combined sounds, but letters are not silently discarded.
4. The new initial clusters **Nd-** and **Nj-**, and the established **Mb-**, are fully articulated:
   - **Nd-**: both *n* and *d* are heard.
   - **Nj-**: both *n* and *j* are heard.
   - **Mb-**: both *m* and *b* are heard.
5. Hyphens used inside the pronunciation guides show audible articulation only. They are not part of the written headword.
6. Unsmooth compounds remain the dictionary headwords. A euphonic form is listed only as an optional spoken alternative.
7. No word below automatically creates an unrestricted family. The three proposed new primitives authorize only the meanings and derivatives explicitly listed here.

## Proposed primitive roots

### `Ndara` — wall

Celan has roots for covering, defense, stone, and binding, but none of those independently names the ordinary upright structural divider found in nearly every built environment. `Ndara` fills that concrete architectural gap without redefining `Xar`, `Arth`, `Mor`, or `Bel`.

Its initial `Nd-` is deliberately articulated. The word is not pronounced *dara*.

Approval would authorize only:

- `Ndara`: wall

It would not automatically authorize words for fence, partition, barricade, rampart, or border wall.

### `Njor` — structural crossing span

Existing passage roots describe going through, along, or toward something. They do not name the load-bearing object that carries passage **over** a gap. `Njor` supplies that missing built concept.

Its initial `Nj-` is deliberately articulated. The word is not pronounced *jor*.

Approval would authorize only:

- `Njor`: bridge or structural crossing span
- `Dronjor`: aqueduct

It would not automatically authorize metaphorical bridges, diplomatic reconciliation, bridge decks, suspension bridges, or anatomical bridges.

### `Ndavi` — vertical level

The existing spatial system can say above and below, but built environments repeatedly require a fixed, countable height position: the third storey, the lower platform level, or the level where an elevator stops. `Ndavi` supplies that neutral coordinate.

Its initial `Nd-` is deliberately articulated. The word is not pronounced *davi*.

Approval would authorize only:

- `Ndavi`: vertical level or height position
- `Ndavior`: storey or building level
- `Ndavivek`: elevator or mechanical lift

## Meaning boundaries and contrast tests

| Pair | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| `Mekor` / `Teremil` | A `Mekor` is any usable building; a `Teremil` is specifically a home. A temple, factory, or station can be a `Mekor` without being a `Teremil`. |
| `Ndara` / `Xar` | `Ndara` is a physical upright structural surface. `Xar` is the broader idea of covering, veiling, or protective enclosure. |
| `Eshxar` / `Aneshxar` | `Eshxar` is the outside roof exposed to weather. `Aneshxar` is the ceiling visible from inside a room. |
| `Morlpral` / `Pralor` | `Morlpral` is the floor of a structure. `Pralor` is a table or ordinary work surface. |
| `Shalshan` / `Vorkor` | `Shalshan` admits light or sight and need not admit a person. `Vorkor` is a door or gateway intended for passage. |
| `Xarwelor` / `Welpralor` | A courtyard is defined by surrounding enclosure. A plaza is defined by public gathering and need not be enclosed. |
| `Xaror` / `Worxaror` | `Xaror` is any room or chamber. `Worxaror` is specifically a large shared interior room. |
| `Velkelor` / `Liankavor` | A library supports reading and consultation. An archive preserves records, including records not intended for ordinary access. One institution may contain both. |
| `Njor` / `Morlshan` | A bridge carries passage across or above a gap. A tunnel carries passage through or beneath an obstruction. |
| `Droshara` / `Awekshara` | `Droshara` is any constructed water channel. `Awekshara` remains the narrower spring-fed canal. |
| `Dronjor` / `Drobel` | An aqueduct is a particular crossing structure carrying water. `Drobel` is the whole water-distribution system. |
| `Drokav` / `Drojormek` | `Drokav` stores water. `Drojormek` visibly directs moving water upward or outward. |
| `Varbelor` / `Eshpralor` | A station is the organized transport facility. A platform is the physical raised surface within or outside it. |
| `Vekxaror` / `Vekpralor` | A garage encloses or shelters vehicles and may support servicing. A parking area merely provides designated temporary standing space. |
| `Ndavi` / `Ndavior` | `Ndavi` is an abstract or stated height level. `Ndavior` is a usable storey within a building. |
| `Mbalan` / `Mbalanmor` | `Mbalan` remains a person who is a pillar of the community. `Mbalanmor` is the literal architectural support; no second displayed sense is added to `Mbalan`. |
| `Nethmek` / `Nethor` | `Nethmek` is the collective category furniture. `Nethor` remains the specific chair or seat. |

## Cadence and articulated-letter review

The batch deliberately broadens Celan's audible palette without treating one regional sound as decoration.

- `Ndara, Njor, Mbalanmor` gives three distinct grounded onsets: **n-d**, **n-j**, and **m-b**.
- `Dronjor` retains the internal **n-j** transition; it must not collapse into *Drojor*.
- `Mbalanmor` retains both the **m** and **b**; it must not collapse into *Balanmor*.
- `Nethmek` retains both **t** and **h**. Speakers who find the cluster difficult may use optional `Nethamek`, where the inserted vowel makes both consonants easier to hear.
- `Brosshara` retains the compound boundary between `Bros` and `Shara`; optional `Brosashara` may be used to prevent the boundary from swallowing a consonant.

The goal is not to make every word begin with `Mb-`, `Nk-`, `Tl-`, `Nd-`, or `Nj-`. It is to let Celan use a wider range of consonant movement where the sound and the meaning both justify it.

## Collision and family audit

- Exact headword scan against the current canonical dictionary found no collision for any of the 40 proposed spellings.
- `Drokav` is retained for cistern/reservoir rather than replaced by a new reservoir word.
- `Awekshara` remains the specialized spring-fed canal beneath the generic proposed `Droshara`.
- `Liankavor` remains archive; `Velkelor` is proposed only for library.
- `Thalorimor` remains the specialized lightning-management structure; `Eshmekor` is the ordinary generic tower.
- `Mbalan` is not given a new dictionary sense. `Mbalanmor` is a separate, narrowly defined physical derivative.
- No declined form such as `Zhirsharaen` or `Zhirsharaaen` appears in this batch.

## Risks requiring explicit review

1. **`Ndara`, `Njor`, and `Ndavi` are new primitives.** Approval would establish only the listed meanings and the explicitly listed derivatives, not unrestricted word families.
2. **`Mbalanmor` makes a literal architectural word from the established social metaphor `Mbalan`.** It must remain a separate headword so the dictionary does not turn `Mbalan` itself into a polysemous “person/pillar” entry during the current expansion phase.
3. **`Nethmek` tests the articulated-letter rule at a difficult compound boundary.** Approve it only if `/neht-hmehk/` feels speakable, with `Nethamek` as the optional euphonic form. If it does not, the furniture category should be renamed before integration.
4. **`Belzhirkavor` is the longest word in the batch.** Its length reflects a genuinely compound institution—system + connected lines + preserved store—but it should be rejected if ordinary speakers would consistently shorten it beyond recognition.
5. **`Velkelor` must not absorb archive.** The library/archive distinction is functional: consultation and circulating texts versus preservation of records.
6. **`Varbelor` deliberately covers both station and terminal.** If Celan later needs a technical distinction between an intermediate stop and a journey endpoint, that should be a later narrower derivative rather than two competing generic words now.
7. **Inherited spellings containing `th` are not globally reanalyzed by this proposal.** This batch applies the no-silent-letter requirement to its own proposed forms; a language-wide decision about the exact phonetic value of inherited `th` would require a separate pronunciation review.

## Approval boundary

Approval of this proposal would authorize exactly **40 universal headwords**. It would not authorize:

- automatic derivatives beyond those listed;
- new grammatical categories;
- national origins for universal vocabulary;
- replacement or exposure of intentionally suppressed older senses;
- a language-wide respelling of inherited words;
- or integration of any declined transportation proposal.

Status: **Audit draft — meanings only; no Celan forms proposed or approved**

Baseline: **726 app headwords / 795 displayed senses**

Audit result: **452 missing universal meanings**

| Priority | Meanings |
|---|---:|
| High | 274 |
| Medium | 168 |
| Low | 10 |
| **Total** | **452** |

The 274 high-priority meanings happen to equal the present numerical distance from 726 to 1,000 app headwords. This is a coincidence, not a finding that exactly 274 new headwords should be admitted. Root reuse and headword-admission review will convert some rows into added senses or transparent phrases and may reject others; it may also reveal that a surviving meaning needs more than one lexical distinction.

## Purpose

This ledger consolidates the twelve-domain profiles for Jasara, Nivveil, Valkeldor, Zarithan, Marakor, Verdalris, Pelagae, and Mechuma. The profiles are treated as scenario generators, not as authorities for Celan spellings, national ownership, proper names, rituals, or unsettled lore.

A meaning remains in this ledger only when the assembled Celan dictionary does not already define the thing. Existing coverage is judged from dictionary definitions and approved senses, including established senses intentionally hidden from the ordinary display. A word occurring only in an example, a proper name, or an unattached corpus form does not by itself establish a dictionary definition.

## Admission boundary

- Include explicit concepts and the immediately necessary concepts required to describe the activity naturally.
- Merge national implementations when they share a universal semantic core.
- Exclude proper names and named rituals.
- Preserve universal offices and roles revealed by culturally specific titles.
- Keep a general category and a common subtype when speakers genuinely need the distinction.
- Treat the magic vocabulary as universal operations and relations. Do not lexicalize named magical schools without fuller lore review.
- Do not force the inventory to equal the 274-headword milestone.
- A row is a missing **meaning**, not an automatic promise of one new headword. Later admission review may select a headword, an added sense, a transparent phrase, or rejection.

Priority key:

- **H** — recurring ordinary need across several scenes or nations
- **M** — clearly useful, but less frequent, more specialized, or plausibly compositional
- **L** — legitimate distinction to retain for later review; likely phrase-level or context-dependent

## 16. Recommended review sequence

The inventory should not be coined all at once. Review it as capability batches of roughly 30–40 meanings:

1. Food production and preparation
2. Clothing, materials, and built space
3. Tools, repair, and transport
4. Government, occupations, and trade actions
5. Defense and survival
6. Recreation, art, and public life
7. Environment and ecology
8. Information and electrical systems
9. Science, astronomy, and cosmology
10. General magic and metaphysical practice

For each batch, the next stage must still perform root reuse, decide headword versus added sense versus transparent phrase, test scene use, check sound and collisions, and obtain explicit approval before integration.

# Food, Agriculture, and Preservation 1

Status: **Approved and integrated — 2026-09-11**

Scope: first of two food-category passes. This pass establishes food production, cultivated places, gathering, hunting, fishing, livestock, and several universal gathered foods. Cooking and preservation processes remain for Pass 2.

All candidates are universal Celan. National origin and national usage remain blank. Proposed euphonic alternatives are optional searchable variants; every primary form is the unsmoothed construction.

## Proposed new primitives

Approval of this batch would authorize only the listed roots and listed derivatives, not automatic families.

### `Sov` — cultivated plot

Closest roots considered: `Mor` soil, `terra` physical land, `Bal` growth, `Pral` cultivation/craft, and `-or` place.

Why they were not sufficient: `Mor` and `terra` include uncultivated ground; `Bal` includes spontaneous growth; `-or` supplies only generic location. Compounds such as `Pralbalor` could describe a growing place but would make every ordinary field subtype long and would not establish the culturally important boundary between managed plot and surrounding land. `Sov` provides that stable center and supports field, terrace, garden, and orchard distinctions.

### `Gav` — pursuit of living quarry

Closest roots considered: `Shara` following a route or person, `Rath` enemy, `Veth` hiddenness, and `Ral` watching or stalking.

Why they were not sufficient: hunting is not merely following, concealment, watching, or treating an animal as an enemy. `Gav` isolates purposeful tracking and pursuit of living quarry. The proposed headwords are the verb `Gavaen` and role noun `Gavral`; the technical root does not require a separate dictionary category.

### `Nesh` — aquatic creature

Closest roots considered: `Mahr` ocean, `Dren` water, `Torin` meat/protein, and the named fish `Lumora`.

Why they were not sufficient: water and ocean are environments, meat is the animal after it becomes sustenance, and `Lumora` is one schooling species. `Nesh` supplies the broader aquatic-creature category. In ordinary unmarked use it can refer to a fish, while compounds distinguish shellfish and later aquatic classes without making a particular species generic.

### `Zor` — animal or beast

Closest roots considered: `Torin` meat/protein, `Rask` hide, `Vershara` a prey animal, and the many named creatures.

Why they were not sufficient: all describe animal products, roles, or particular species rather than a living animal in general. `Zor` supplies the category needed for livestock, herding, milk, ecology, veterinary care, and later animal vocabulary.

### `Nelar` — nectar

Closest roots considered: `Vilor` small sweet fruit, `Tarin` dried sweetness, `Jel` flavor, and `Dren` liquid.

Why they were not sufficient: none distinguishes sweet liquid produced directly by a plant. The earlier one-syllable proposal `Nek` has been withdrawn because established `Nekvar` “Good morning” would invite a false compound analysis even though `Nek` is not an attested root of that greeting. Two-syllable `Nelar` avoids that collision, provides a stable natural substance, and allows honey to be defined transparently as animal-transformed nectar.

## Root-reuse and collision review

1. `Balpral`, `Balral`, `Pralbal`, and `Balpralaen` deliberately reuse the existing growth-and-craft system. Their order matters: `Balpral` is growth-practice, while `Pralbal` is the living growth produced by deliberate practice. Neither changes `Bal` from general sprouting nor `Bren` from harvest/yield.
2. `Drobalaen` uses approved ordinary physical-water `Dro`, not symbolically broader `Dren`. It names supplying water to growth and does not replace `Drobel`, the water-distribution system that may perform that work.
3. `Brossov`, `Verdsov`, and `Vilorsov` are independently useful managed-place types. They are not automatic authorization for every possible `Sov` compound.
4. `Kesbrenaen` distinguishes attentive searching-and-gathering from `Bren`, the gathered harvest or yield. It reuses the recently approved `Kes` on equal footing with older roots.
5. `Gav` avoids using `Rath` “enemy” for prey and avoids generalizing `Vethral`, an established pack hunter/predator term, into every human act of hunting.
6. `Nesh` does not replace `Lumora`, `Drenlivar`, or `Jekvarin`. Those remain a schooling fish, salted fish, and spiral shellfish respectively. `Xarnesh` creates the needed generic shellfish category through the culturally intelligible “covered aquatic creature” model.
7. `Mahrfennil` names marine greens themselves. `Eshtenor` remains a particular prepared seaweed snack.
8. `Zor` is simply the ordinary general word for an animal or beast, while `Torin`, `Rask`, and `Mekrask` remain meat/protein, raw hide, and leather. `Belzor` requires domestication and continuing care; wild animals are not livestock.
9. `Belzoraen` is coordinated animal care and movement, not magical binding or social coercion. `Belzorral` is broader than a shepherd of one particular species.
10. `Zordren` is milk as a biological liquid. It does not make every animal-derived liquid milk. `Zordrenvinaen` explicitly adds extraction so the noun and action remain distinct.
11. `Felverd` and `Morlverd` use ordinary visible resemblance without redefining all small plants as moss or all stone growth as lichen. Their narrow lexical definitions must remain attached to these full compounds.
12. `Nelar` and `Zornelar` distinguish nectar from honey. `Vilor`, `Tarin`, and named sweet drinks keep their established meanings. `Nekvar` remains the unanalyzed established greeting “Good morning” and receives no new derivation.
13. `Jelkor` is a flavoring material. `Jel` remains flavor itself, while `Belar`, `Belmor`, `Jekvor`, `Vethhar`, and other established entries remain particular spices.
14. `Fennildren` is an infused plant drink. It does not replace named tea varieties. `Emildren` is the broader prepared-beverage category and does not replace physical water `Dro`, drinking `Drenaen`, or particular drinks.
15. Exact scanning found no current headword or stored variant matching any proposed primary or euphonic form.

## Deferred from Pass 1

- `Wheat` remains deferred rather than being reverse-engineered from color or geography. `Lak` already supplies grain, and a specific wheat term should be reviewed with flour, dough, and cereal distinctions.
- Cooking oil, fat, ingredient, recipe, cooking processes, freshness, spoilage, and preservation actions belong in Pass 2.
- Approval of this pass does not authorize any unlisted derivatives of its five approved roots.

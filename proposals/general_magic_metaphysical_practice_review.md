# General Magic and Metaphysical Practice

## Revised Combined Lexical Review — Passes 1 and 2

**Status:** Approved and integrated  
**Scope:** Universal Celan vocabulary; no national origins assigned  
**Canon effect:** 53 new headwords and 53 displayed senses  
**Current dictionary baseline:** 1,297 app headwords and 1,375 displayed senses  
**Integrated dictionary result:** 1,350 app headwords and 1,428 displayed senses; 0 validation warnings  
**Revision reason:** The first proposal overused `Aru` as a generic compound element. This revision retains `Aru` only for magic in general, three top-level magical domains, and a few concepts that genuinely require the umbrella meaning. Specific practices now grow from the established roots that carry their actual metaphors.

## Executive finding

Magic in Ohnosha is easier to describe when three separate questions are kept apart:

1. **What domain does the magic act upon?** Spiritual, physical, or mental.
2. **How is it practiced?** Through channeling, attunement, spell-patterns, binding, shaping, ritual, communion, machinery, or another method.
3. **What does it produce or cost?** Protection, containment, illusion, compulsion, transformation, exhaustion, instability, backlash, or another consequence.

The three domains are analytical descriptions, not new dictionary categories and not exclusive schools:

- **`Rinaru` — spiritual magic:** essence, spirits, attunement, spiritual bonds, and metaphysical identity.
- **`Kalaru` — physical magic:** matter, bodies, elements, energy, force, motion, and physically manifested change.
- **`Esharu` — mental magic:** thought, awareness, perception, memory, emotion, dream, and will.

A practice may belong to more than one. Biomancy may be physical when it changes tissue and spiritual when it changes essence. An illusion may alter a person's mind, project actual light, or do both. Technomancy describes an interface with constructed systems, not a fourth magical substance.

## What changed from the first proposal

The first proposal attached `Aru` to nearly every practice, effect, artifact, and consequence. That produced a tidy derivational matrix but made the language monotonous and concealed Celan's existing metaphors.

This revision:

- removes `Arukor`, `Arushan`, `Arupral`, `Arutal`, `Belaru`, `Artharu`, `Xararu`, `Vaararu`, `Ratharu`, `Aruvek`, `Arupel`, `Arushen`, all elemental `-aru` formations, and all `Aru-` exhaustion terms;
- uses `Rin`, `Thaen`, and `Ohm` for spiritual practice;
- uses `Kal`, `Mor`, `Shan`, `Pral`, and individual elemental roots for physical practice;
- uses `Esh`, `Kes`, `Vel`, `Veth`, and `Thar` for mental practice;
- leaves attunement, ritual, healing, temporal stabilization, anomaly handling, magical detection, and many other meanings with their already established words;
- treats transparent phrases as a strength rather than lexicalizing every mechanically possible specialization.

`Aru` now appears in 12 of the 53 candidates: five members of the basic umbrella family, three primary domain labels, the spell pair, general dispelling, and technomancy. The remaining 41 candidates develop through other Celan roots.

## Existing coverage retained

| Existing word or family | Coverage retained |
|---|---|
| `Terra` | The living world and Heart of Terra; one possible source or relationship, not the definition of magic |
| `Zhel` | Energy, spark, and charge; some magic uses energy, but magic is not universally reducible to energy |
| `Ohm`, `Ohm'aen`, `Ohm'rin`, `Ohmaen` | Connection and attunement; no duplicate attunement family is proposed |
| `Rin` | Essence, inner spirit, and true self; the spiritual domain develops from this meaning without replacing it |
| `Thaen` | Spirit-breath and living spiritual quality; `Thaenin` adds an independently agentive being without changing the root sense |
| `Khumeth` | Sacred ritual or ceremony; ritual may be magical or nonmagical |
| `Lianaen` | Prayer or speaking truth toward the divine, Heart, or Balance; prayer is not automatically spellcasting |
| `Rinvarash` | Prophetic vision or spiritual insight |
| `Arth`, `Arthkal` | Protection and a physical, carried, worn, generated, or projected shield; `Arthrin` is specifically an essence-held ward |
| `Xar` | Covering, concealment, closure, and enclosure; `Xarrin` is a metaphysically held seal |
| `Kes`, `Kesmek` | Personal noticing and a sensor capable of detecting magical conditions; `kes aru` remains sufficient for ordinary magical detection |
| `Shanartheth` | Insulation against transfer, including magical energy; not immunity to every kind of magic |
| `Felkal` | Bodily fatigue; magical causation can be stated as `felkal thal aru` without another long headword |
| `Vethvelrathor` | An Echo without agency; categorically distinct from `Thaenin` |
| `Drenvaraen` | Change or transformation; magical transformation remains expressible without a specialized long compound |
| `Zhael`, `Morzhael`, `Talzhael` | Renewal, physical healing, and defined treatment; healing does not become inherently magical |
| `Thalnoraen`, `Lianthalaen`, `Belkoraen` | Temporal stabilization, anomaly stabilization, and anchoring; no duplicate magical versions are proposed |
| Named rites | Every existing proper or national rite retains its identity and is not generalized or overwritten |

## Productive phrases deliberately preferred over new headwords

- magical source: `kor thal aru` or a direct phrase naming the actual source
- magical capacity: use `Kal` with the person, artifact, place, or system under discussion
- magical intensity: measure the relevant `Kal`, `Zhel`, effect, field, or condition rather than assuming one universal unit
- magical exhaustion: `felkal thal aru`, fatigue from magic
- cosmic magic: `aru songaeth`
- temporal magic: use `Aru`, `Nor`, and the specific temporal action; existing Post-Fracture terms already describe most operational needs
- spatial magic: use `Aru`, `Shan`, `Shara`, and the actual crossing or boundary effect
- healing magic: combine `Aru` with `Zhael`, `Morzhael`, or `Talzhael` according to whether the speaker means renewal, recovery, or treatment
- magical transformation: use `Drenvaraen` with the affected substance or state
- magical detection: `kes aru` for personal noticing; `Kesmek` for an instrument capable of detecting a magical condition
- sand, stone, metal, gravity, sound, weather, memory, dream, and emotion shaping: use the domain verb or a transparent phrase unless repeated scenes later prove that a separate lexicalized headword is needed

## Primitive-root review — `Aru`

The active inventory was tested before retaining the new primitive:

- `Terra` would make the Heart of Terra or living world the universal definition of magic.
- `Zhel` would classify all magic as energy or charge.
- `Ohm` would classify all magic as attunement or connection.
- `Rin` and `Thaen` cannot honestly cover physical, technological, temporal, spatial, or fracture-derived practice.
- `Khumeth` is ceremony, one method among many.
- `Kal` includes ordinary bodily, mechanical, political, and physical power.

`Aru` remains justified as the neutral umbrella because Ohnoshans need to ask whether something is magical before they know its source, method, domain, or explanation. Its family is deliberately constrained. Approval would authorize only the 12 `Aru`-bearing headwords explicitly listed here, not an automatic compound system.

## Explicit review points

1. **Three-domain model:** `Rinaru`, `Kalaru`, and `Esharu` are overlapping descriptions, not new app categories or exclusive schools.
2. **Mental `Esh`:** `Esharu` lexicalizes the established thought and intellect range of `Esh`; `Eshpralaen` lexicalizes its air and wind range. Their complete forms, not the English gloss alone, determine the meaning.
3. **Spell as defined expression:** `Arukel` treats a spell as a repeatable defined pattern, whether expressed by voice, movement, mark, music, calculation, or tool.
4. **Enchantment as essence-binding:** `Rinbel` moves enchantment away from the generic magic root and toward Celan's internal metaphor of binding an operative identity or property.
5. **Spirit agency:** `Thaenin` names an agentive spirit-being without declaring it dead, ancestral, divine, or trustworthy; the Echo boundary remains absolute.
6. **Method-neutral mental effects:** Illusion, compulsion, and perceptual veiling can be magical, technological, chemical, ritual, or psychological. Context identifies the method.
7. **Elemental verbs rather than “-mancy” nouns:** The proposal prioritizes actions people perform and the materials they affect instead of mechanically naming every possible school.
8. **Consequences are not one fuel meter:** Fatigue, overchanneling, instability, backlash, and disrupted attunement remain distinct conditions.

## Recommendation

This 53-headword revision is substantially stronger than the first proposal. It keeps one needed umbrella root while allowing the inherited language to do most of the semantic work. The result sounds less templated, preserves Ohnosha's plural metaphysics, and gives speakers several ways to explain the same magical event according to domain, method, expression, and consequence.

Until explicit approval, this report remains a proposal and changes nothing in the Canon Dictionary.

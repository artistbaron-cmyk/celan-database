# Sex, Birth, and Death — Batch 1

**Status:** Approved and integrated  
**Scope:** Universal Celan vocabulary  
**Integration state:** Integrated as `LX-SBD1-0001` through `LX-SBD1-0009` after explicit user approval on 2026-09-12.

## Approved headwords

| Celan | Category | Pronunciation | Meaning | Derivation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Tlamor` | Noun | /tlah-mohr/ | Sex or sexual activity | `Tla` intimacy + `Mor` physical embodiment |
| `Tlamoraen` | Verb | /tlah-moh-rah-ehn/ | To have sex or engage sexually | `Tlamor` + `-aen` |
| `Tlamoreth` | Adjective | /tlah-moh-rehth/ | Sexual; pertaining to sexual activity or function | `Tlamor` + `-eth` |
| `Shalbal` | Noun | /shahl-bahl/ | Birth; emergence into independent life | `Shal` life or beginning + `Bal` budding growth |
| `Shalbalaen` | Verb | /shahl-bah-lah-ehn/ | To give birth; passive `nor-ka` means to be born | `Shalbal` + `-aen` |
| `Shalbaleth` | Adjective | /shahl-bah-lehth/ | Newborn or newly born | `Shalbal` + `-eth` |
| `Duma` | Noun | /doo-mah/ | Death; irreversible end of bodily life | New two-syllable primitive |
| `Dumaen` | Verb | /doo-mah-ehn/ | To die | `Duma` + `-aen` |
| `Dumeth` | Adjective | /doo-mehth/ | Dead or deceased | `Duma` + `-eth` |

## Boundaries

- `Tlamor` does not automatically mean love, marriage, reproduction, consent status, biological sex classification, or participant gender.
- `Shalbal` covers compatible human, nonhuman, and magical modes of emergence into independent life.
- `Duma` describes bodily death without deciding the fate of soul, identity, memory, or afterlife.
- Pregnancy, conception, sexual desire, orgasm, miscarriage, stillbirth, killing, murder, corpse, funeral, burial, and mourning remain outside this batch.
- `Shalabal` and `Shalabaleth` are optional euphonic forms, not separate headwords.
- Only the nine listed headwords are authorized; no automatic derivative families are approved.

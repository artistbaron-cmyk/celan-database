# Science, Astronomy, and Cosmology

## Combined Lexical Review Report — Passes 1 and 2

**Status:** Approved and integrated  
**Scope:** Universal Celan vocabulary; no national origins assigned  
**Canon effect:** 55 new headwords and 55 displayed senses  
**Previous dictionary baseline:** 1,242 app headwords and 1,320 displayed senses  
**Dictionary after integration:** 1,297 app headwords and 1,375 displayed senses  
**Review method:** Whole Canon Dictionary reconciliation, active-root reuse, exact-headword collision check, hidden-root policy review, Ohnoshan Metaphor Test, world-fit review, spoken cadence testing, register control, and headword-admission review

## Executive finding

Celan already possesses much of the conceptual foundation required for science. It can name matter, force, energy, gravity, atmosphere, temperature, measurement, standards, calibration, sensors, gauges, laboratories, observatories, computation, data, records, causes, results, uncertainty, attempts, and failure. Those meanings should remain intact.

The missing layer is not “knowledge” in general. It is the language that lets speakers distinguish:

- the universe from the sky and outer space;
- stars, suns, planets, systems, galaxies, constellations, orbits, and satellites;
- eclipses, asteroids, comets, meteoroids, meteors, and meteorites;
- dust, vacuum, and mass from generic matter;
- science from scholarship, truth, wisdom, and computation;
- ordinary noticing from formal observation;
- evidence, experiments, hypotheses, theories, models, simulations, samples, variables, controls, probability, uncertainty, accuracy, precision, prediction, and replication;
- a lens, telescope, and microscope from a generic sensor or measuring instrument.

The category divides naturally into two passes:

- **Pass 1 — Astronomy, Cosmology, and Physical Space:** 27 approved headwords.
- **Pass 2 — Scientific Inquiry, Testing, and Instruments:** 28 approved headwords.
- **Three new primitive roots are approved:** `Songa`, universe or cosmos; `Nyel`, star; and `Vora`, planet.
- **No additive senses were added to existing headwords.**
- **No new dictionary categories were created.**

These words are universal overall. Zarithan celestial navigation, Pelagaean tidal observation, Nivveilian aurora study, Marakorian formal analysis, Verdalrisian living observation, and Mechuman instrumentation expose different applications of the same vocabulary. None of those uses makes a nation the originator of the words.

## Ohnosha-fit and mystery boundary

This proposal gives Ohnoshans language for studying the world without making one worldview canonically correct.

- `Songashen`, cosmology, names disciplined inquiry into the universe as a whole. It does not establish whether the Heart of Terra is a being, divine gift, planetary force, engineered mechanism, or something else.
- `Belkes`, scientific theory, is a revisable explanatory framework supported by evidence and successful testing. It is neither a casual guess nor an absolute truth.
- `Sivkel`, hypothesis, is testable and provisional. Religious, poetic, philosophical, and political claims do not become scientific hypotheses merely because someone calls them explanations.
- `Kesdava`, evidence, may support, weaken, or fail to decide between claims. Evidence is not automatically truth.
- `Rinmek`, model, and `Varrinmek`, simulation, are representations. They are not the reality represented.
- `Songaeth`, cosmic, marks relation to the scale or structure of the universe. It does not identify a species, moral rank, or divine status.
- `Kreznyel`, sun, is a common noun for a star functioning as a world's primary source of daylight and heat. The proposal does not decide how many suns exist throughout the universe or impose a proper name on Ohnosha's local sun.

## Pass 1 semantic separations

- `Dral` remains the experienced sky or heavens. `Songazun` is outer space. `Songa` is the entire universe. None of the new universe-scale meanings is assigned to `Dral`.
- `Esh` is ordinary air or sky. `Arshor` is an atmosphere. `Zunesh` is a vacuum, including one made inside a chamber.
- `Nyel` is any star. `Kreznyel` is a star understood as a sun in relation to a world. `Shalithor` remains sunlight received from a sun.
- `Terra` is the sacred living-world concept and the Heart of Terra; `terra` is physical earth or land; `Vora` is the astronomical class planet; `Ohnosha` remains the world's proper name.
- `Lun` already means moon. `Luneth` merely supplies the independently useful adjective lunar.
- `Nyelzhir` is a pattern recognized from an observer's perspective. `Nyelbel` is a physically associated star system. `Wornyelbel` is a galaxy containing enormous numbers of stars and systems.
- `Ravwek` is a body's recurring path under sustained attraction. `Dralshara` remains a route laid out through celestial references for travelers or navigators.
- `Ravwekin` is the orbital role and therefore includes moons and constructed satellites. It does not erase the specific word `Lun`.
- `Dralxar` is the event or condition called an eclipse. Ordinary covering and closing remain in the `Xar` family.
- `Felvora` is an asteroid-sized orbiting body. `Zhirnyel` is the traditional appearance-based “thread-star” term for a comet and does not classify it scientifically as a star. `Dralfel` is a smaller traveling fragment associated with the heavens, `Krezdralfel` the luminous atmospheric passage, and `Mordralfel` the material that reaches the ground.
- `Felmor` is fine particulate matter whether terrestrial, biological, industrial, or cosmic. `Gorm` remains unwanted dirt or grime; `Felsadar` remains fine mineral sediment or silt.
- `Morshen` is mass. Weight remains the force experienced under gravity and can be expressed through `Shen`, `Kal`, `Kalrav`, or an appropriate measured construction.
- `Dralshen` studies the observable heavens and celestial processes. `Songashen` asks universe-scale structural and historical questions. They overlap without making sky and universe synonymous.

## Primitive-root review for Pass 1

### `Songa` — universe or cosmos

The existing inventory has the experienced heavens (`Dral`), air or sky (`Esh`), the living world (`Terra`), physical matter (`Mor`), and organized or shared wholes (`Bel`, `Wel`). None means the total physical expanse containing sky, worlds, matter, energy, and events.

- `Dral` was rejected because heaven or sky is a perspective-bound celestial domain, not everything that exists physically.
- `Terra` was rejected because it is world- or planet-centered and carries sacred Heart-of-Terra meaning.
- `Wel` and `Bel` were rejected because togetherness and organization do not themselves establish universe-scale totality.

`Songa` therefore earns an independent two-syllable semantic center. Approval authorizes only `Songa`, `Songaeth`, `Songazun`, and `Songashen` from this report, not an unrestricted family.

### `Nyel` — star

Celan has light (`Shal`), fire (`Krez`), and heavens (`Dral`), but an individual star is neither generic light, ordinary fire, nor the entire sky. Making every star term a `Dral` compound would force the sky root to serve simultaneously as place, object, classifier, and universe.

`Nyel` supplies the short everyday astronomical noun from which the independently useful stellar, sun, system, galaxy, and appearance-based comet forms can be built. Approval authorizes only `Nyel`, `Nyeleth`, `Kreznyel`, `Nyelbel`, `Wornyelbel`, `Nyelzhir`, and `Zhirnyel` from this report.

### `Vora` — planet

`Terra` cannot safely become the generic scientific word for every planet without weakening its established sacred and world-specific role. `Mor` is matter generally, and `Dral` merely locates something in the heavens without distinguishing a planet from another celestial object.

`Vora` supplies the astronomical category planet without overwriting `Terra`, `terra`, or `Ohnosha`. Approval authorizes only `Vora`, `Voraeth`, and `Felvora` from this report.

## Pass 2 semantic separations

- `Shenbel` is the organized practice and body of revisable inquiry. `Lian` remains truth or natural law; `Vel` remains wisdom or deep memory; `Raku` remains computation; `Shenral` remains the researcher or scholar.
- `Kes` is noticing. `Shenkes` is one bounded formal observation. `Keshen` remains a clinical examination, and `Mekkes` remains a technical diagnostic examination.
- `Kesdava` is evidence relevant to evaluating a claim. `Shendava` is data generally. A piece of data becomes evidence only in relation to a question or claim.
- `Siv` is any attempt under uncertainty. `Sivshen` is a controlled experiment designed to yield interpretable evidence.
- `Sivkel` is provisional and testable. `Belkes` is a larger explanatory framework supported across observations and tests. `Lian` should not be attached merely to make either sound certain.
- `Rinmek` selectively represents. `Varrinmek` makes such a representation operate or change through a sequence. Neither is an exact duplicate of reality.
- `Felshen` can be a material specimen, biological specimen, sampled reading, or selected population subset. Context supplies the domain; the stable center is a bounded selection used to learn about more than itself.
- `Drenvar` names what may change. `Drenvaraen` remains the general verb to change or become meaningfully different.
- `Sivshenlian` is the comparison condition inside an experiment. `Shenlian` remains the broader standard or reference measure.
- `Rethshen` measures likelihood of an event or outcome. `Shenreth` states uncertainty surrounding a measurement. Root order carries the distinction.
- `Shenlianeth` concerns closeness to a reliable reference. `Pelsheneth` concerns narrowness and repeatability. A result may be precise but inaccurate, or accurate but imprecise.
- `Shenvarash` is an evidence-derived prediction. `Varash` retains future or prophecy, including non-scientific future vision.
- `Weksivshen` is deliberate repetition for verification. `Wek'aen` remains ordinary “again” or “repeat,” and `Belwek` remains an ordered recurring process.
- `Shalpel` is the light-directing component. `Drallor` and `Fellor` are complete observation instruments. `Kesmek` remains any sensor and `Shenmek` any gauge or meter.

| Science and method | `Shen`, `Bel`, `Kes`, `Siv`, `Kel` | Measurement, organization, observation, uncertain testing, and statements form a coherent method vocabulary | `Lian` is protected from becoming a claim that every scientific proposal is truth |
| Evidence and information | `Kes`, `Dava` | Evidence is information anchored to observation and evaluative relevance | `Shendava` remains data generally |
| Models and simulations | `Rin`, `Mek`, `Var` | Selected essential structure is constructed, then made to change or operate | Does not redefine copies, reality, artificial persons, or computation |
| Samples, variables, and controls | `Fel`, `Shen`, `Drenvar`, `Lian` | Bounded selection, changeable factor, and reliable experimental reference stay distinct | No duplicate words for ingredient, portion, ordinary change, or standard |
| Probability and measurement quality | `Reth`, `Shen`, `Lian`, `Pel` | Root order separates likelihood from measurement uncertainty; reference and fine resolution separate accuracy from precision | Does not turn uncertainty into failure or confusion into falsehood |
| Prediction and replication | `Shen`, `Varash`, `Wek`, `Sivshen` | Marks evidence-based future claims and purposeful verification by repetition | Prophecy and ordinary repetition remain available |
| Optical instruments | `Shal`, `Pel`, `Dral`, `Fel`, `Lor` | Light, focusing, celestial direction, small scale, and sight yield compact functional words | Does not overuse `Mek`, and does not restrict instruments to glass or electronics |

## Cadence review

The candidates were tested in mixed ordinary and technical sequences:

```text
shenkesaen shenral nyel shan Drallor; kavdavaen la an shenkes.
The researcher observes a star through a telescope; they record the observation.

sivshenaen shenral felshen ser sivshenlian; rinaen kesdava an belkes.
The researcher experiments with a sample and a control; the evidence supports the theory.

ravwekaen Lun an Vora; shenvarashaen shenral an dralxar.
The moon orbits the planet; the researcher predicts the eclipse.

varrinmekaen mekral an zhelbel; rethshen zhirath meketh rinaen fel.
The engineer simulates the power grid; the probability of system failure is small.
```

These are cadence tests rather than new canonical grammar examples. Capitalization in final examples should follow whether a speaker means a proper or archetypal body, and the grammar should receive its normal integration validation if the batch is approved.

## Risks requiring explicit review

1. **`Songa`, `Nyel`, and `Vora` are new primitives.** They were approved because `Dral` means heaven or sky—not universe, star, and planet simultaneously. Approval authorizes only the three meanings and explicitly listed derivatives in this report.
2. **`Kreznyel` defines sun relationally rather than as one unique proper object.** This supports other planets and multiple-star systems without inventing facts about Ohnosha. A local proper name may still be established later.
3. **`Nyelzhir` makes constellations recognized “star-thread” patterns rather than physically bound star groups.** This preserves room for different national sky traditions without creating different words for the same visible stars.
4. **`Belkes` deliberately gives scientific theory more weight than hypothesis but less finality than `Lian`.** Approval should confirm that organized observation is an appropriate Celan metaphor for an evidence-supported explanatory framework.
5. **`Rinmek` and `Varrinmek` use essence plus construction to distinguish model from simulation.** They must remain representations and must not automatically acquire meanings such as artificial person, duplicate being, or constructed soul.
6. **`Felshen` unifies material sample, specimen, sampled reading, and population sample around bounded representative selection.** Context must reliably distinguish the substance or population sampled.
7. **`Rethshen` and `Shenreth` depend on root order.** The first measures uncertainty as likelihood; the second states uncertainty in a measurement. Their contrast should be approved or rejected as a pair.
8. **`Shenlianeth` and `Pelsheneth` introduce the accuracy/precision distinction.** Their usefulness is high in technical speech, but ordinary speakers may use broader phrases when the distinction does not matter.
9. **`Zhirnyel` is a historically transparent “thread-star” word for comet.** Its meaning follows visible appearance and ordinary naming history; scientific speakers do not thereby classify a comet as a kind of star.
10. **`Drallor` and `Fellor` are compact lexicalized instrument compounds without `Mek`.** This avoids unnecessary length; both written `l` sounds remain pronounced, and optional Euphony forms are listed.

## Deferred deliberately

- A proper name for Ohnosha's sun, galaxy, or star system.
- A canonical count of suns, moons, planets, dimensions, or inhabited worlds.
- A final creation model, origin of Day One, or proven nature of the Heart of Terra.
- Astrology, divination, and religious cosmography; those require their own boundary work with magic and ritual.
- Detailed chemical taxonomy, atomic particles, genetics, cybernetics, and artificial intelligence.
- Discipline-by-discipline names for physics, chemistry, biology, geology, meteorology, and medicine. Existing compounds and context cover the immediate scenes; a later academic-fields pass may add terms only where institutions repeatedly require them.
- Units beyond the established Celan measurement system. This report supplies concepts, not a replacement measurement standard.

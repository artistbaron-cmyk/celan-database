# Organized Conflict — Proposal 1

**Status:** Approved and integrated  
**Scope:** Universal Celan vocabulary  
**Target layer:** Military organizations, patrols, sieges, traps and targets, combat actions, poison, war, and battle  
**Dependency:** `Ngarwel` and `Ngarjoraen` depend on approval of proposed primitive `Ngar` in Defense and Weapons Proposal 1  
**Integration state:** Integrated as `LX-OC1-0001` through `LX-OC1-0014` after explicit user approval on 2026-09-12.

## Design commitments for this batch

1. Existing conflict vocabulary remains primary where it already works. `Jorkal` stays charge or attack, `Arth` stays protect or defend, and `Belshara` already supplies tactic or coordinated maneuver.
2. All proposed meanings are universal overall, so national origin and national usage should remain blank if approved.
3. No new primitive root is proposed in this pass. Two forms conditionally reuse proposed `Ngar`; all other forms reuse the active approved inventory.
4. The batch uses only existing Noun and Verb categories.
5. `Bel` appears only in `Vethbel`, where literal binding or restraint is essential to the meaning of a trap.
6. Unsmoothed forms remain primary. Listed alternatives are optional euphonic variants of the same entries.
7. Approval would authorize only the fourteen listed headwords and meanings, not their automatic derivative families.

## Meaning boundaries and contrast tests

| Pair | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| `Welarth` / `Ngarwel` | A militia is rooted in local community defense and commonly consists of people who retain civilian roles. An army is a formally commanded force organized for sustained armed operations. Either may be permanent or temporary, but their organizing principles differ. |
| `Ngarwel` / `Serenkal` | An army is an organization. A `Serenkal` is one elite front-line warrior or member of a vanguard. |
| `Kesvar` / ordinary travel | A patrol travels in order to watch, deter, warn, or secure. Repeated travel or commuting without that security purpose is not a patrol. |
| `Xarkaleth` / `Arthlor` | A siege is an operation imposed around a fortified place. `Arthlor` is the defensive formation or fortified position resisting or preparing for attack. |
| `Vethbel` / `Rathvethor` | A trap is a prepared mechanism or situation intended to act on a target. `Rathvethor` is an unseen enemy, ambush, or hidden threat and may exist without any trap. |
| `Lorpel` / `Rath` | A target is selected for directed attention or action and need not be hostile. `Rath` is an enemy or foe whether or not currently targeted. |
| `Lorpelaen` / `Lorin` | Aiming aligns toward one selected target or point. `Lorin` guides a person, group, process, or route more broadly. |
| `Ngarjoraen` / `Jorkal` | Firing launches a projectile or discharge from a weapon. `Jorkal` is the broader offensive charge or assault and need not involve ranged weapons. |
| `Arthkaraen` / `Arth` | Blocking or parrying intercepts one incoming attack. `Arth` covers protection and defense generally, including preparation, sheltering, guarding, and sustained defense. |
| `Vethvaraen` / flight | A retreat is purposeful withdrawal that preserves organization or future action. Uncontrolled panic or merely running away is not necessarily a retreat. |
| `Kaarthaen` / `Ka` or `Verka` | Surrender is an action that ends resistance and accepts opposing control. `Ka` and `Verka` remain ordinary refusal or emphatic negation. |
| `Mavmor` / `Mav` | Poison is the harmful substance. `Mav` remains the condition of bodily illness it may cause. |
| `Rathnor` / `Kelreth` | War is sustained organized armed conflict. A dispute may be verbal, legal, economic, or political without armed violence. |
| `Rathjor` / `Jorkal` | A battle is the whole bounded encounter involving opposing forces. `Jorkal` is one attack, assault, or forceful push within it. |
| `Rathjor` / `Rathnor` | A battle is one bounded encounter. War is the larger sustained organized conflict and may contain many battles or long periods without battle. |

## Root-reuse review

- **Militia:** `Wel` supplies community participation and `Arth` shared defense. `Seren` was rejected because militia names the organization, not merely a collection of professional guardians.
- **Army:** proposed `Ngar` supplies armed function and `Wel` collective coordination. `Serenin` was rejected because `-in` is ordinary grammatical pluralization, not an institution-building suffix. `Bel` was not needed.
- **Patrol:** `Kes` gives deliberate attention and `Var` purposeful movement. The whole word lexicalizes the recurring security journey rather than every observant walk.
- **Siege:** `Xar` supplies enclosure and restricted passage, while `Kaleth` supplies the fortified objective. `Rathlor` was rejected because danger around a fortress is not necessarily a sustained siege.
- **Trap:** `Veth` gives concealment or unseen operation and `Bel` literal binding or restraint. `Vethdor` was considered but rejected because hidden placement alone does not explain capture, restraint, injury, or disabling action.
- **Target and aiming:** `Lor` supplies directed focus and sightline; `Pel` supplies a precise point. This pairing supports attack, observation, tools, sensors, and magic without treating every target as an enemy.
- **Firing:** proposed `Ngar` keeps the action weapon-bound, while `Jor` supplies projectile or discharge release. `Krez` was rejected because firing need not involve flame or heat.
- **Blocking:** `Arth` gives defense and `Kar` the incoming strike or applied force. The whole verb is event-specific and does not replace general protection.
- **Retreat:** established `Vethvar` already means behind or toward the rear relative to facing or motion. Verbalizing it supplies purposeful rearward withdrawal without inventing a retreat root.
- **Surrender:** `Ka` supplies cessation or the without-state and `Arth` active defense or resistance. The whole word lexicalizes formal yielding; it does not make every lapse in protection a surrender.
- **Poison:** approved `Mav` provides bodily illness and `Mor` physical substance. `Aiv` was rejected because some toxins disrupt thought, breathing, or organ function before producing pain.
- **War:** `Rath` provides hostile opposition and `Nor` its continuation through time. `Bel` was considered for military organization but rejected because war is defined more reliably by sustained armed conflict than by one bound institutional structure.
- **Battle:** `Rath` provides hostile opposition and `Jor` the bounded forceful clash. The form remains distinct from `Rathorjor`, an established memory intrusion.
- **Tactic:** no new construction is justified because `Belshara` already names a coordinated maneuver or tactical path.

## Cadence and lineage review

- `Welarth, Kesvar, Vethbel, Lorpel` provide compact two-beat operational vocabulary.
- Proposed `Ngarwel` and `Ngarjoraen` extend the weapon root only where armed organization or weapon discharge is indispensable.
- `Xarkaleth` and `Vethvaraen` are longer but remain common three-part rhythms with clearly audible root boundaries.
- The repeated `Rath-` onset in `Rathnor` and `Rathjor` creates an intelligible conflict-level contrast: sustained war through time versus one forceful battle.
- `Mavmor` reuses one of the newer bodily-life roots, keeping later expansion connected to recently approved vocabulary rather than defaulting to the oldest productive roots.
- No inherited African-influenced form is added cosmetically. The proposed `Ng-` lineage is extended only in the two meanings that genuinely require the proposed weapon category.

## Collision and family audit

- Exact dictionary scanning found no collision for the fourteen primary spellings or ten optional euphonic forms.
- `Welarth` does not add an organizational sense to `Arth` or a defensive sense to `Wel`.
- `Vethbel` uses `Bel` literally and does not alter `Belshara`, `Belvok`, or the binding root itself.
- `Lorpel` and `Lorpelaen` do not alter `Lorin`, `Pel`, or the physical tool family.
- `Vethvaraen` derives from displayed `Vethvar` and does not replace the established preposition.
- `Kaarthaen` does not alter ordinary negation, refusal, protection, or defense.
- `Mavmor` does not add poison as a second meaning of illness.
- `Rathjor` remains visually and phonologically distinct from `Rathorjor` memory intrusion.
- Optional euphonic forms would remain variants and would not increase the headword count.

## Risks requiring explicit review

1. **Two entries depend on proposed `Ngar`.** `Ngarwel` and `Ngarjoraen` cannot enter canon unless the generic weapon root is also approved.
2. **`Kesvar` allows activity/group polysemy.** Like English patrol, it can name the watch journey or the group conducting it. If Celan should force those apart, the definition must be narrowed before approval.
3. **`Vethbel` is the batch's one new `Bel` compound.** It is justified specifically as concealed binding or restraint and should not revive `Bel` as generic filler.
4. **`Lorpel` is intentionally non-hostile.** A sensor, investigator, healer, hunter, or fighter may all select a target; the word does not imply intent to harm.
5. **`Kaarthaen` is more than ceasing to defend.** Its whole-word meaning includes submission to opposing control; temporary lowered defense does not qualify.
6. **`Mavmor` covers harmful biological or chemical substances broadly.** Ordinary medicine is not poison merely because a harmful dose is possible; context and harmful function remain central.
7. **`Rathnor` combines hostility with duration.** It must not make every long feud, disagreement, rivalry, or hostile feeling a war; organized armed conflict remains essential.
8. **Tactic receives no new headword.** Approval would affirm that existing `Belshara` is adequate for a specific coordinated maneuver or tactical path while preserving all its current displayed senses.
9. **`Rathnor` is a deliberate near-neighbor of `Rathor` past/history.** The medial `n` is fully pronounced: /rahth-nor/ versus /rah-thor/. The semantic relationship to conflict sustained through time is useful, but the pair should be rejected if that spoken distinction feels too narrow.

## Approval boundary

Approval of this proposal would authorize exactly **fourteen universal headwords**, **ten optional euphonic variants**, and confirmation that `Belshara` already covers tactic. It would not authorize:

- canonical integration before explicit approval;
- approval of `Ngarwel` or `Ngarjoraen` without the `Ngar` primitive;
- any national origin or national usage field;
- a new dictionary category or primitive root;
- automatic derivative families;
- ranks, military branches, command structures, units, campaigns, occupations, imprisonment, wounds, killing, victory, defeat, or peace treaties;
- a generic violence meaning for `Jorkal` beyond its established charge or attack sense;
- or replacement of any existing displayed definition.

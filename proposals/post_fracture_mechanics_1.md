# Post-Fracture Mechanics 1

Status: **Approved — integrated into canon**

Current size: **40 candidates**.

Purpose: give Celan speakers practical language for timeline sickness, temporal distortion, cross-timeline memory, localized reality anomalies, abnormal gravity, field measurement, marking, avoidance, and stabilization after Day One.

This batch does not create Celan replacements for the proper names *The Wound*, *the Veilwrought*, *the Vengeance Stone*, or *the Shard*. It also leaves the progressive stages of *The Diminishing* for a separate candidate batch. Generic shards remain in the established `Xil` family.

All candidates are intended as universal Celan. National origin and national usage should remain blank unless later review identifies a genuinely nation-specific term. Categories use only existing dictionary categories. Optional forms are always euphonic variants; the unsmoothed construction remains the proposed primary headword.

## Working metaphysical distinctions

- A timeline is a continuous thread of events, not merely time in the abstract.
- Timeline sickness is an abnormal condition affecting a person, place, or bounded domain through damaged temporal continuity.
- A regular cycle is not a recursive time loop. `Weknor` remains ordinary cyclical time; the proposed loop term adds disorder.
- An Echo is a residual, non-agential memory-pattern. It is not a spirit, intentional ghost, dream, hallucination, or ordinary memory.
- Cross-timeline memories are authentic memories whose events belong to another timeline.
- A reality anomaly is a failure of coherent physical or temporal law, not deception, uncertainty alone, or ordinary chaos.
- Gravitational pull is physical force. It remains distinct from emotional yearning and destiny attraction even though it reuses `Rav` under the physical-force root `Kal`.

## Root-reuse audit

No new primitive root is proposed in this batch.

- Time language reuses `Nor`, `Zhir`, `Thal`, `Reth`, `Xil`, `Fel`, `Del`, `Wek`, `Jor`, `Vel`, and `Shan`. `Rathor` was not used for the timeline itself because it points toward the past, history, and particular remembered events rather than the full continuing event-thread.
- Timeline sickness reuses the recently approved `Mav` illness root. A separate temporal-disease primitive would duplicate an already productive distinction.
- Cross-timeline memory reuses `Velrathor` and `Velzhiraen`, while `Shan` marks passage across temporal states. Ordinary remembering and ritual remembrance remain untouched.
- The Echo term reuses `Veth` and `Velrathor`. `nor-ka` was rejected because it is already the passive echo particle; `Vethrin` was rejected because it already means dream; `Velrathor` alone remains ordinary memory.
- Reality-anomaly terms reuse `Lian`, `Reth`, `Thal`, `Mor`, `Xar`, and `Or`. `Rethvok` was rejected as the generic base because it means chaos or disorder broadly rather than failure of reality's laws.
- Gravity terms reuse `Kal` and `Rav`. `Rav` alone remains yearning, destiny attraction, or general pull; `Kal` constrains the compound to physical force and weight. `Vin` was rejected because it centers extraction or drawing something out from an inner source.
- Field language reuses approved `Dor` and established `Kel`, `Vaar`, `Shen`, `Bel`, `Kor`, `Ka`, and `Shara` rather than inventing roots for marking, clearance, anchoring, or avoidance.

Several requested capabilities remain transparent phrases rather than new headwords:

- Detect an anomaly: use approved `Kes` with `Lianreth`.
- Measure an anomaly: use existing `Shen` with `Lianreth`; `Lianrethshen` names the resulting reading.
- Enter or cross an anomaly boundary: use `Var` or approved prepositional `Shan` with `Lianrethxar`.
- Escape or leave: use ordinary movement with `Thal` “from/out of” rather than creating an anomaly-specific escape verb.
- Heavy or light under changed gravity: use established `Maganeth` and `Feleth` unless repeated scenes require narrower adjectives.

## Risks requiring explicit review

1. `Vethvelrathor` lexicalizes “shadow-memory” as the precise non-agential Echo defined for *The Diminishing*. Its lack of thought, volition, and spirit is part of the word's approved meaning if accepted; it must not become a generic ghost term.
2. `Felnor` uses diminishment to mean a missing experienced interval, not merely a brief amount of time. Approve only if this feels like a natural post-Fracture extension of `Fel`.
3. `Delnor` means two time states co-present in one place or experience, not any pair of dates or times.
4. `Jornor` and `Velnor` establish surge/stillness as the fast-time and slow-time contrast. They should be reviewed as a pair.
5. `Shanvelrathor` and `Shanvelzhiraen` reserve `Shan` for memories that genuinely cross between timelines, not ordinary recollection or imagination.
6. `Belvelrathor` is involuntary memory entanglement. It does not replace the intentional ritual memory-weaving of `Bel'Rathorimeth`.
7. `Lianreth` treats reality as natural law becoming disordered. It must not mean lying, illusion, doubt, or generic chaos.
8. `Lianrethxar` uses cover/veil language for a perceptible enclosing boundary. It does not imply that every anomaly is sealed or physically impassable.
9. `Kalrav` deliberately narrows `Rav` through `Kal` so the compound means physical gravitational attraction rather than desire or destiny.
10. `Kasharaen` lexicalizes a path-without-contact metaphor as ordinary avoidance. It should work beyond anomaly scenes without becoming a general refusal verb.
11. `Belkoraen` uses binding-to-core for metaphysical or physical anchoring. It does not mean holding something in the hand; approved `Korinaen` retains that meaning.
12. The six proposed euphonic forms are optional alternatives only. The unsmoothed forms are the proposed headwords.
13. This batch authorizes no new primitive roots and no automatic derivative families.
14. Proper lore names remain excluded. Acceptance of this batch would not create Celan translations for *The Wound*, *the Veilwrought*, *the Vengeance Stone*, or *the Shard*.

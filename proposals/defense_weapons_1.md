# Defense and Weapons — Proposal 1

**Status:** Approved and integrated  
**Scope:** Universal Celan vocabulary  
**Target layer:** Generic weapons, common hand weapons, projectile weapons, firearms, energy weapons, and ammunition  
**Integration state:** Integrated as `LX-DW1-0001` through `LX-DW1-0009` after explicit user approval on 2026-09-12.

## Design commitments for this batch

1. Existing defensive and tool vocabulary remains unchanged. `Peleth` stays knife or blade, `Arthlor` stays defensive formation or shield wall, `Arthaxar` stays armor, and `Karmek` stays an ordinary tool.
2. All meanings are universal overall, so national origin and national usage should remain blank if approved.
3. The batch uses only the existing Noun category.
4. One new primitive, `Ngar`, is proposed because the active inventory has no honest general category for a weapon.
5. Approval would authorize only `Ngar` and the eight explicitly listed compounds, not an automatic weapon family.
6. Every letter contributes audibly. Initial `Ng-` in `Ngar` is pronounced as an articulated nasal-plus-stop onset, represented here as /n-gahr/.
7. Unsmoothed compounds remain the primary headwords. Listed alternates are optional euphonic forms of the same entries.

## Meaning boundaries and contrast tests

| Pair | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| `Ngar` / `Karmek` | A weapon is defined by harmful or coercive use. A tool is defined by practical work. An object improvised as a weapon can be called `Ngar` in that scene without permanently ceasing to be a tool. |
| `Ngarpel` / `Peleth` | `Ngarpel` is a sword: a long combat blade with a hilt or equivalent hand grip. `Peleth` remains a knife or blade and can describe a kitchen knife, pruning knife, or the cutting part of another implement. |
| `Ngarzhir` / `Zhirpel` | `Ngarzhir` is a long shafted weapon. Established `Zhirpel` is a needle: a fine pointed implement associated with thread and detailed work. |
| `Ravjor` / `Wekrav` | A bow stores and releases tension to launch an arrow. `Wekrav` remains a rotating pulley that redirects pull through a flexible line. |
| `Ravjorvek` / `Ngarvek` | Every arrow can function as ammunition, but `Ravjorvek` names the specific shafted projectile of a bow. `Ngarvek` is the broader ammunition category. |
| `Arthkal` / `Arthlor` | A shield is one portable, worn, generated, or projected barrier. `Arthlor` is a coordinated defensive formation, shield wall, or fortified position. |
| `Arthkal` / `Arthaxar` | A shield intercepts attack as an external barrier. Armor is fitted protective covering that remains on a person or object. |
| `Ngarjor` / `Ngarzhel` | A gun launches material projectiles through controlled propulsion. An energy weapon directs non-material energy. A hybrid device may truthfully be described by either term according to what it fires. |
| `Ngarvek` / `Zhelkav` | Ammunition is consumed or discharged in weapon use. `Zhelkav` remains a general battery or power cell and is not ammunition unless deliberately supplied as a weapon's firing charge. |

## Root-reuse review

- **Generic weapon:** `Kar` was considered because it covers striking and applied force, but established `Karmek` already means an ordinary tool and many weapons do not strike through direct mechanical contact. `Rath` was rejected because a weapon can be used against an animal, structure, machine, or non-enemy target. `Arth` was rejected because weapons are not inherently defensive. The recurring category therefore justifies the proposed primitive `Ngar`.
- **Sword:** `Peleth` remains too broad and too ordinary: it covers knives and blades used in kitchens, agriculture, and craft. `Ngarpel` preserves the blade relationship while lexicalizing the long combat weapon.
- **Spear:** `Zhir` already supports thin linear objects such as wire and the calibrated rod in `Shenzhir`. With `Ngar`, it supplies a long shafted weapon without turning `Zhirpel` needle into a weapon.
- **Bow:** `Rav` provides stored pull or tension and `Jor` the forceful release. `Ngar` is unnecessary here because the whole `Ravjor` construction already isolates the weapon's defining mechanism cleanly.
- **Arrow:** `Vek` supplies a carried or propelled unit beneath the established `Ravjor` bow. `Pel` was considered but rejected from the compound because an arrow remains an arrow even with a blunt practice head.
- **Shield:** `Arth` provides protective purpose and `Kal` force-bearing resistance. `Xar` was rejected because a shield need not cover or conceal the bearer, and `-or` was rejected because `Arthlor` already names a defensive place or formation.
- **Gun:** `Jor` supplies the contained burst that propels material ammunition. `Krez` was rejected because not all guns depend on fire or heat, and `Mek` was omitted to keep the frequent word compact after `Ngar` and `Jor` already establish a constructed firing weapon.
- **Energy weapon:** `Zhel` supplies directed energy or charge. The whole word remains weapon-specific through `Ngar`; it does not convert batteries, grids, or conductive materials into weapons.
- **Ammunition:** `Vek` supplies the loaded or delivered firing unit. `Mor` was rejected because ammunition can be an energy charge rather than material shot, while `Zhel` was rejected because arrows and cartridges need not themselves be energy cells.

## Cadence and lineage review

- `Ngar` adds a compact voiced onset that is not already represented by the newer `Nd-` and `Nj-` families. Its sound is meaning-bearing rather than decorative because it anchors a genuinely missing productive category.
- The initial sequence is articulated: /n-gahr/, not an English-style silent-letter spelling. Speakers may realize the transition smoothly, but both the nasal and stop contribute audibly.
- `Ngar, Ngarpel, Ngarzhir, Ngarjor, Ngarzhel, Ngarvek` form a recognizable family without repeating one long bureaucratic compound.
- `Ravjor` and `Arthkal` keep the batch from becoming mechanically uniform and preserve older root lineages where their physical meanings are more exact.
- Spoken together, `Ngarpel, Ngarzhir, Ravjor, Arthkal` alternate grounded clusters with open vowel releases and do not collapse into a single repetitive cadence.

## Collision and family audit

- Exact dictionary scanning found no collision for the nine primary spellings or eight optional euphonic forms.
- `Ngar` does not occur as an existing headword, root, variant, or proper name in the assembled project data.
- `Ngarpel` does not change or add a sense to `Peleth`.
- `Arthkal` does not change the displayed meanings of `Arth`, `Arthlor`, or `Arthaxar`.
- `Ngarvek` does not change `Vek`, `Zhelvek`, or any vehicle word.
- Optional euphonic forms would remain searchable variants and would not increase the headword count.

## Risks requiring explicit review

1. **`Ngar` establishes a productive weapon category.** Approval should authorize only the primitive meaning and eight listed compounds, not every mechanically possible `Ngar-` formation.
2. **Initial `Ng-` adds a new articulated onset.** Both written consonants must remain audible; approval would not impose this onset on unrelated vocabulary.
3. **`Ngarzhir` relies on the linear-form range of `Zhir`.** The whole word means spear, but approval must not redefine standalone `Zhir` as a shaft or weapon.
4. **`Ravjor` lexicalizes one mechanism as bow.** It should not become a general word for tension, springs, slings, or launchers.
5. **`Arthkal` includes physical and projected shields.** Its stable center is an external attack-intercepting barrier, not every protective field, wall, shelter, or item of armor.
6. **`Ngarjor` excludes directed-energy weapons.** Devices that primarily emit electrical, magical, kinetic, or radiant energy belong under `Ngarzhel` even when English speakers casually call them guns.
7. **`Ngarvek` is a functional category.** Arrows, cartridges, projectiles, and firing charges can all be ammunition; ordinary batteries remain `Zhelkav` unless deliberately serving as a consumable weapon charge.

## Approval boundary

Approval of this proposal would authorize exactly **nine universal headwords**, **one new primitive root**, and **eight optional euphonic variants**. It would not authorize:

- canonical integration before explicit approval;
- any national origin or national usage field;
- a new dictionary category;
- automatic derivative families;
- new verbs for aiming, shooting, blocking, drawing, loading, or reloading;
- specific firearm classes such as pistol, rifle, shotgun, cannon, or launcher;
- specific ammunition classes such as bullet, cartridge, shell, magazine, quiver, or power pack;
- military organizations, patrols, battles, war, tactics, retreat, or surrender;
- or replacement of any existing displayed definition.

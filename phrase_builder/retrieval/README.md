# Retrieval Packs

This folder holds compact support packs for the Phrase Builder.

These are not meant to be full archives. They are short, focused reference pages that gather the most useful material around one phrase family or meaning neighborhood.

The goal is simple:

- when a phrase comes in
- the builder should be able to look toward a small, relevant support pack
- instead of wandering across the whole project at random

## What a retrieval pack should include

Each pack should usually contain:

- what kind of phrase family it covers
- what the builder should notice first
- a few canon-supported phrase examples
- the core concept fields involved
- the key grammar habits involved
- cultural voice notes if they matter

## What this folder is for

This folder is the first real step toward making the Phrase Builder active.

It does not make the builder intelligent by itself.

What it does do is give the system a small, usable place to look before it answers.

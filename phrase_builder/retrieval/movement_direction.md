# Movement and Direction Retrieval Pack

## Phrase family

Going, heading, guiding, arriving, traveling, seeking direction, and path-centered phrasing.

## What to notice first

Movement is one of the clearest structural centers of Celan.

The builder should notice whether the English phrase is:

- simple movement
- a direction question
- guidance
- purposeful travel
- flowing or wandering movement

## Strong phrasebank examples

- `ra var ser?`
  - Where does the friend go?
  - Notes: direction question through the movement verb

- `ra var evan emil dren?`
  - Where is the market?
  - Notes: navigation and location-seeking through movement language

- `lorin dren morl.`
  - The mountain guides the water.
  - Notes: guidance language and patterned movement

- `var I belshara.`
  - I must go.
  - Notes: purpose-driven movement with modality

## Concept fields involved

- `Action and Movement`
  - `Var`
  - `Shara`
  - `Lorin`
  - `Min`
  - `Shalil`

- `Truth, Balance, and Order`
  - `Thal` may matter when movement is aligned, balanced, or rightful

## Grammar habits involved

- VSO is especially visible in movement lines
- movement verbs often anchor direction questions
- modality can follow the subject rather than changing the verb shape
- destination and accompaniment often depend on prepositions and order

## Cultural voice notes

- Pelagaean movement may feel more flowing
- Zarithan movement may sound direct and practical
- Valkeldorian movement may feel purposeful and firm
- Jasaran movement may sound balanced and elegant

## Useful builder reminder

Do not force movement into English-style location logic if Celan would more naturally treat the phrase as path, going, or guidance.

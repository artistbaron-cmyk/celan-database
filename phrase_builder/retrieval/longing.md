# Longing Retrieval Pack

## Phrase family

Longing, yearning, desire, intimate wanting, and heart-pulled expression.

## What to notice first

Celan does not treat longing as one single narrow word.

This family often spreads across:

- desire
- yearning
- heart-centered intention
- emotional pull
- longing for presence, place, or connection

The builder should notice whether the English phrase is:

- simple wanting
- deep yearning
- intimate desire
- longing for presence
- longing for a place, person, or state

## Strong phrasebank examples

- `Theraen La Shalor.`
  - I yearn for the Sanctuary.
  - Notes: deep, spiritual longing

- `ohmaen I sharaka.`
  - I love freedom.
  - Notes: value-charged desire or devotion, not just preference

- `aen thar lian.`
  - The heart speaks truth.
  - Notes: not a longing line by itself, but useful because Celan often routes deeper desire through the heart

## Grammar habits involved

- desire can be expressed through heart-centered modifiers such as `thar-ka`
- longing may need reshaping if the English phrase is highly idiomatic
- VSO structure still holds even when the emotional force is strong
- not every English intimacy phrase should be translated literally at the body-part level

## Cultural voice notes

- Pelagaean and Verdalrisian voices may sound more fluid, relational, or image-rich
- Zarithan and Valkeldorian renderings may sound more direct and less ornamental
- Marakorian longing may feel formal or elevated rather than openly raw

## Useful builder reminder

Do not reduce longing to a bland "want" if the phrase clearly carries yearning, ache, nearness, or inward pull.

# Blessings Retrieval Pack

## Phrase family

Blessings, parting words, spiritual goodwill, and elevated hopes for another person.

## What to notice first

Celan blessings often do not sound like blunt statements.

They tend to:

- lean hopeful or reverent
- use light, peace, path, or heart-centered imagery
- allow elevated phrasing without changing the core grammar

The builder should notice whether the English phrase is:

- a blessing
- a wish
- a gentle invocation
- a formal goodwill phrase

## Strong phrasebank examples

- `vael vaar an shara-ya.`
  - May peace be on your path.
  - Notes: strong journey blessing; hopeful and elevated

- `shal lorin ser.`
  - Let the light guide you.
  - Notes: spiritual guidance; reverent tone

- `va var kalor thal!`
  - Go in strength and balance!
  - Notes: blessing shaped through command form; empowering rather than harsh

## Grammar habits involved

- `vael` often helps carry hope, wish, or elevated possibility
- VSO movement still matters even in blessings
- commands can soften into ceremonial or encouraging speech depending on tone
- writing conventions may allow meaningful capitalization if the blessing invokes an archetypal force

## Cultural voice notes

- Verdalrisian and Pelagaean voices may sound more fluid or lyrical
- Valkeldorian blessings may feel firmer or more strength-centered
- Nivveilian blessings may be restrained but still weighty

## Useful builder reminder

Do not flatten blessings into plain English-style statements if Celan would more naturally shape them as wishes, path-language, peace-language, or guidance-language.

# Truth-Speaking Retrieval Pack

## Phrase family

Truth, honesty, declaration, moral speech, aligned speech, and statements about order or rightness.

## What to notice first

Truth in Celan is often larger than factual correctness.

The builder should notice whether the English phrase is about:

- honesty
- moral clarity
- aligned speech
- order or legitimacy
- heartfelt truth

## Strong phrasebank examples

- `aen thar lian.`
  - The heart speaks truth.
  - Notes: sincerity and inward truth

- `Lorin belthal Ilin.`
  - Order guides us.
  - Notes: truth through rightful structure and law

- `li-ya, nor aen lian?`
  - May we now speak truth?
  - Notes: entering a truth-bearing register through respectful speech

## Grammar habits involved

- truth-speaking may be plain, but it often carries elevated moral force
- respectful entry into truth-speaking may change the social texture of the phrase
- Celan may route truth through heart, order, or balance rather than using only flat assertion language

## Cultural voice notes

- Mechuman truth-speech may sound exact and structural
- Verdalrisian truth-speech may sound luminous or inward
- Marakorian truth-speech may feel formal and weight-bearing

## Useful builder reminder

Do not assume “truth” always means bare fact. In Celan, truth may also mean right relation, moral alignment, or speech that properly names reality.

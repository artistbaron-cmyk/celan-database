# Peace, Relief, and Safety Concept Pack

## Concept field

Peace, Relief, and Safety

## Core idea

This field gathers peace, calm, relief, communal ease, safety, and inward settling. It is not one of the largest fields in the language, but it carries real weight in blessing, reassurance, and emotional resolution.

## Anchor forms

- `Vaar` — peace; calm; relief; goodness
- `Wel` — sharing; unity; communal seamlessness
- `Nkuthan` — resolved calm; inward settledness

## What this field naturally supports

- blessings
- reassurance
- peaceful arrival
- communal ease
- emotional settling
- safety language

## What the builder should notice

- Is the phrase about peace as a state, or peace as shared unity?
- Is the calm inward, communal, or protective?
- Would this line land better as blessing, reassurance, or statement of settledness?

## Common danger

Do not assume peace in Celan means only the absence of conflict. It can also mean goodness, safe settlement, shared ease, and inward resolution.

# Truth, Balance, and Order Concept Pack

## Concept field

Truth, Balance, and Order

## Core idea

This field gathers truth, harmony, reciprocity, legitimacy, woven structure, and the sense that things are rightly placed. In Celan, truth is often ethical and cosmological, not only factual.

## Anchor forms

- `Lian` — truth; reality; what is aligned with what is real
- `Thal` — balance; harmony; equilibrium
- `Bel` — bind; weave; structure; ordered joining
- `Belthal` — order; established harmony; rightful structure

## What this field naturally supports

- truth-speaking
- moral statements
- aligned or rightful action
- social order
- cosmic or philosophical balance

## What the builder should notice

- Is the phrase about bare fact, or about rightness?
- Is the speaker naming order, legitimacy, or harmony rather than only truth?
- Would the phrase sound more natural if routed through order, balance, or heart-truth instead of blunt assertion?

## Common danger

Do not reduce `truth` to a purely informational English meaning if the phrase is clearly dealing with moral order, rightful structure, or aligned speech.

# Longing Concept Pack

## Concept field

Heart, Desire, and Longing

## Core idea

Longing in Celan is not a thin want-word. It lives across heart, desire, yearning, inner pull, and the sense that a person is being drawn toward someone, somewhere, or something.

## Anchor forms

- `Thar` — heart; inward emotional center
- `Thar-ka` — desire; wanting by heart's pull
- `Rav-` — intensified desire; driven wanting
- `Ravokhaen` — to desire strongly; to want intensely
- `Theraen` — to yearn for; to long for

## What this field naturally supports

- yearning phrases
- intimate desire
- wanting to see or be near someone
- spiritual longing
- emotionally driven movement or decision

## What the builder should notice

- Is this simple wanting, or deeper yearning?
- Is the phrase intimate, spiritual, or practical?
- Is English using a body-image idiom that Celan would reshape into heart, presence, or desire language?

## Common danger

Do not flatten longing into a generic “want” if the phrase clearly carries ache, inward pull, nearness, or yearning.

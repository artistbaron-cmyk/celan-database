# Concept Packs

These files are the deeper meaning packs for the Phrase Builder.

The blended retrieval packs are useful because they gather phrase examples, concept cues, and grammar habits in one place. These concept packs go one level deeper.

Their job is to answer:

- what kind of meaning-world is this
- which roots anchor it
- what kinds of phrases it naturally supports
- what mistakes the builder should avoid when working inside it

## What these packs are for

Use these when a phrase clearly lives inside a strong meaning field, such as:

- longing
- truth and order
- movement
- relation
- grief
- peace

They are not full archives. They are compact semantic footholds.

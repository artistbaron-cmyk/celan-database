# Action and Movement Concept Pack

## Concept field

Action and Movement

## Core idea

This field gathers doing, going, heading, guiding, carrying, direction, and path. It is especially important because Celan often lets action organize meaning first, both structurally and emotionally.

## Anchor forms

- `Var` — go; move; proceed
- `Shara` — path; journey; way
- `Lorin` — guide; lead
- `Min` — head over; wander; move casually
- `Shalil` — flow; move fluidly

## What this field naturally supports

- ordinary movement
- direction questions
- travel
- guidance
- purposeful motion
- path-centered metaphors

## What the builder should notice

- Is this phrase really about location, or about going?
- Is the movement direct, fluid, guided, casual, or destiny-shaped?
- Does the English sentence want a place answer, while Celan would naturally frame it through path or motion?

## Common danger

Do not force movement into static English-style location logic if Celan would more naturally make action or path the center of the phrase.

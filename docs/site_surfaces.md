# Site Surfaces

This page explains the main parts of the visible app.

## Dictionary

The Dictionary is the lexicon-facing surface.

Its job is to let people find words, see how they are used, and understand their place in the language without getting buried in backstage structure.

## Grammar Guide

The Grammar Guide is the learner-facing grammar book inside the app.

Its current structure is built around the main lesson pages rather than old rule shards.

The core sections are:

- Pronunciation
- Sentence Shape
- Word Building
- Possession and Relation
- Number and Measure
- Gender, Address, and Social Use
- Writing Conventions
- Cultural Voice

Its job is to teach Celan in a shaped, readable way rather than behaving like a raw rule browser.

## Phrase Builder

The Phrase Builder is the most unfinished major surface.

Its current interface is intentionally simple:

- phrase input
- cultural lens
- send
- translation
- explanation

The current limitation is not the interface. The limitation is the depth of the phrase-shaping logic underneath it.

That is why so much backstage work is happening in the Phrase Builder support layer.

## How to think about the site

Right now:

- Dictionary is strong
- Grammar Guide is strong
- Phrase Builder is promising but still under construction

That is the honest state of the visible product.

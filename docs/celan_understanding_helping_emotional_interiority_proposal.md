# Celan Proposal: Understanding, Helping, and Emotional Interiority

**Status:** Approved and integrated into canon on 2026-09-15  
**Scope:** Universal Celan  
**Proposed package:** 21 dictionary headwords, 2 searchable colloquialisms, and 25 ordinary phrase patterns  
**Canon decision:** ED-0032

## Purpose

This proposal fills two connected gaps:

1. the ability to negotiate understanding and help in ordinary conversation;
2. the ability to name six common emotional states without collapsing them into fear, anger, shame, sadness, desire, or solitude.

The design principle is ordinary use. These forms must work between friends, children and caregivers, coworkers, healers, teachers, strangers, and civic workers. They should not require ritual language or an extended elemental metaphor merely to say what someone feels or needs.

The proposal does not attempt to create one Celan equivalent for every related English word. Each candidate has a narrow semantic center, while ordinary phrases handle tense, intensity, cause, and social context.

# Part I — Understanding and Helping

## What Celan already has

Celan is not empty in this area:

- `Khum-` concerns listening, knowing through hearing, patient attention, and introspection.
- `Kes` means noticing or directing attention toward something.
- `Sel` has an approved mental sense: clarity or an unobscured, internally ordered state of thought.
- `Lian` concerns truth, reality, honesty, and genuine quality.
- `Kel` concerns words, speech, naming, definition, and text.
- `Reth` concerns confusion, uncertainty, doubt, and disorder.
- `Lia'ka!` means “Aha! Got it!” as a sudden reaction.
- `Lia'ser` means “Right,” “Okay,” “I see,” or “Understood” as an acknowledgment.
- `Ra belnoraen Ilin?` asks whether people are synchronized, following the same plan, or ready together. It does not mean general comprehension.
- `Mb-` is the established root of support, pillars, foundational weight, and people who hold others up.
- `Vaarshan` means rescue from immediate danger; `Zhaelpralaen` means repair; `Nkuraeth` is attested as comfort. None is the ordinary verb “help.”

What remains missing is a general cognitive grasp, a wrong grasp, the acts of explaining and clarifying, and ordinary practical assistance.

| UH-03 | `Rethnem` | /rehth-nehm/ | Noun | A misunderstanding: an interpretation or grasp that is coherent to the person but incorrect | `Reth` uncertainty/disorder + `Nem` understanding |

| UH-07 | `Mbaen` | /m-bah-ehn/ | Verb | To help or assist: to contribute effort, ability, care, or resources toward another's need or undertaking without necessarily taking it over | established `Mb-` support + `-aen` |

### Optional euphonic forms

- `Rethanem` /reh-thah-nehm/ is the optional smoother form of `Rethnem`.
- `Rethanemaen` /reh-thah-nehm-ah-ehn/ is the optional smoother form of `Rethnemaen`.

These would be variants of their unsmoothed headwords, not additional entries.

## Why `Nem` earns a primitive root

The closest existing roots each cover only one neighboring idea:

| Existing root | Why it is not general understanding |
|---|---|
| `Khum` | Patient listening and knowing through hearing; it would make visual, practical, mathematical, and inferred understanding sound hearing-dependent or contemplative. |
| `Kes` | Noticing or detecting something does not mean comprehending it. |
| `Sel` | Mental clarity describes the condition of thought; a person may think clearly and still not understand a subject. |
| `Lian` | Truth describes the status of a claim or reality, not whether a person grasps it. A person can understand a lie. |
| `Vel` | Wisdom is accumulated judgment, not immediate comprehension. |

`Nem` therefore supplies a reusable cognitive center without redefining any established root. It can support comprehension across conversation, craft, education, medicine, navigation, civic procedure, and technical work.

## Why the help verb reuses `Mb-`

Helping does not require another primitive. `Mb-` already expresses support and the bearing of weight. `Mbaen` turns that established relationship into an ordinary action.

Its boundary matters:

- `Mbaen` is general assistance.
- `Vaarshanaen` is rescue from danger.
- `Zhaelpralaen` is repair of an object or system.
- bodily healing retains its own vocabulary.
- comfort is emotional support and does not automatically mean practical help.

## Ordinary phrase patterns

These are speech tests and teaching patterns, not proposed dictionary headwords.

| Celan | Natural meaning | Function |
|---|---|---|
| `Ra nemaen Ya?` | Do you understand? | neutral comprehension check |
| `Nemaen I.` | I understand. | direct confirmation |
| `Ver nemaen I.` | I don't understand. | direct conversational repair |
| `Nemaen I fel.` | I understand a little. | partial comprehension |
| `Lia'ser.` | Understood; okay. | existing acknowledgment; does not necessarily claim deep comprehension |
| `Lia'ka!` | Aha—I got it! | existing sudden realization |
| `Va kelnemaen Ya La wek.` | Explain it again. | request for renewed explanation |
| `Va selnemaen Ya kel.` | Clarify what was said. | request to remove ambiguity |
| `Rethnemaen Ya I.` | You misunderstood me. | correction without claiming deliberate deception |
| `Ra mbaen I Ya?` | May I help you? | ordinary offer of help |
| `Va mbaen Ya I.` | Help me. | direct request |
| `Mbaen an I nor-ka belshara.` | I need help; I must be helped. | need expressed through necessity plus the existing passive construction |
| `Lia. Va mbaen Ya I.` | Yes—help me. | accepting offered help |
| `Ka. Ver mbaen Ya I.` | No—don't help me. | neutral refusal of offered help |

The last four deliberately avoid creating separate headwords for *offer help*, *accept help*, *refuse help*, and *need help*. Celan can construct those meanings naturally once it has `Mbaen`.

## Proposed searchable colloquialisms

| Expression | Pronunciation | Natural meaning | Literal force | Register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Nem ka.` | /nehm kah/ | That makes no sense; I am not following. | Understanding: none. | Informal; direct |
| `Ra mbaen?` | /rah m-bah-ehn/ | Want help? / Need a hand? | Help? | Informal; cooperative |

`Nem ka` is blunter than `Ver nemaen I`: the first can criticize the explanation or situation, while the second reports the speaker's own lack of understanding.

# Part II — Emotional Interiority

## Existing emotional territory that must remain intact

| Existing Celan | Current territory |
|---|---|
| `Fah` / `Fahaen` | fear; to fear |
| `Reth` / `Rethaen` | uncertainty or confusion; to worry or be uncertain |
| `Krez` / `Krezaen` | fire or heat; to rage or burn with anger |
| `Nkathal` / `Nkathalaen` | sadness or grief; to grieve or be sad |
| `Thera` / `Theraen` | longing or yearning; to long for |
| `Ravokha` / `Ravokhaen` | intense desire; to desire intensely |
| `Veth'tharaen` | to feel shame or guilt |
| `Neth` | rest, physical stillness, or support |
| `Vaar` | peace, calm, relief, or safety |
| `Ohm` | connection, empathy, unity, or attunement |

The proposed emotions occupy spaces between these established meanings. They do not replace them.

## The six distinctions

### 1. Envy is not jealousy

- **Envy:** I painfully want an advantage, possession, opportunity, quality, or position that someone else has.
- **Jealousy:** I fear that a valued bond, attention, or place in a relationship may be displaced by another.

One can envy a stranger's skill without fearing the loss of a relationship. One can feel jealous without wanting anything the perceived rival possesses.

### 2. Embarrassment is not shame or guilt

- **Embarrassment:** immediate self-conscious discomfort from exposure, awkwardness, a mistake, or unwanted attention.
- **Shame:** a painful negative judgment of the self or one's social standing.
- **Guilt:** distress connected to something one believes one did wrong.

Embarrassment can be brief, harmless, and even funny. `Veth'tharaen` remains the stronger shame/guilt verb.

### 3. Boredom is not rest or calm

- **Boredom:** restless or unpleasant lack of engagement, meaningful activity, novelty, or attention.
- **Rest:** desired physical stillness.
- **Calm:** absence of agitation or danger.

A person can be peacefully inactive without being bored, or bored while physically busy.

### 4. Loneliness is not physical solitude

- **Loneliness:** painful desire for connection that feels absent or unreachable.
- **Solitude:** being physically alone, which may be chosen and pleasant.

Someone can feel lonely in a crowd and connected while physically alone.

### 5. Resentment is not immediate anger

- **Anger/rage:** a present hot reaction.
- **Resentment:** anger, grievance, or injured regard preserved across time because a harm feels unanswered.

This distinction grows naturally from Ohnoshan experience with preservation and unresolved obligations, but its core meaning is universal.

### 6. Anxiety is not ordinary worry or immediate fear

- **Worry (`Rethaen`):** active concern or uncertainty, often about an identifiable matter.
- **Fear (`Fahaen`):** response to danger or a feared object.
- **Anxiety:** sustained or recurring apprehensive activation under uncertain or anticipated threat, sometimes without a clearly identifiable danger.

The proposed Celan compound joins fear with uncertainty rather than treating anxiety as merely “more worry.”

### Optional euphonic forms

- `Kavakrez` /kah-vah-krehz/ is the optional smoother form of `Kavkrez`.
- `Kavakrezaen` /kah-vah-krehz-ah-ehn/ is the optional smoother form of `Kavkrezaen`.

## Primitive-root review

### `Zev` — envy

Roots considered:

- `Rav` and `Thera` express desire or yearning, but not comparison with another person's possession or advantage.
- `Fah` expresses fear, which belongs to jealousy rather than envy.
- `Shen` could evoke measured shares, but envy is not limited to material allocation or quantity.

Envy therefore needs a semantic center of its own. `Zev` can later support only genuinely needed derivatives such as *envious*; no automatic family is proposed.

### `Mira` — embarrassment

Roots considered:

- `Veth'tharaen` is already shame/guilt and is too morally or personally heavy.
- `Hav` includes laughter caused by embarrassment, but laughter is a possible response, not the emotion itself.
- `Reth` covers confusion or uncertainty but not social exposure.
- a “shadowed face” compound would manufacture a face metaphor not yet shown to be an ordinary Ohnoshan way of naming embarrassment.

`Mira` keeps embarrassment light enough for everyday mistakes while allowing serious context when needed.

### `Daku` — boredom

Roots considered:

- `Neth` is rest and physical stillness; using it would imply that rest is inherently unpleasant.
- `Vaar` is calm or peace and must not be made negative.
- `Ka` plus play, work, or novelty would describe one possible cause of boredom, not the emotion itself.

`Daku` names under-engagement without treating stillness, leisure, or peace as defects.

## Why the other four emotions use compounds

- `Fahohm` is intelligible as fear concerning connection, which cleanly distinguishes jealousy from envy.
- `Ravohm` is the pull toward missing connection, which allows loneliness in company and connection in solitude.
- `Kavkrez` is anger kept across time. This is grounded in ordinary preservation and heat management rather than sacred imagery.
- `Fahreth` is fear shaped by uncertainty. It remains distinct from both immediate fear and ordinary problem-focused worry.

## Existing expressions that already support this field

These should remain available and searchable; they are not new proposals:

- `Kavdava ka.` — “No record.” Playful denial of an embarrassing event or demand for proof.
- `Vekaen La dren kaveth.` — “They carry old water.” To revive or continue carrying an old grievance. This is elevated language and does not replace the ordinary proposed `Kavkrez` family.
- `Wek'aen aenthael.` — “The song repeats.” A familiar, weary, or teasing response to hearing the same excuse or complaint again.

# Cadence and collision review

The proposed set was tested as a spoken group:

> `Nem. Nemaen. Rethnemaen. Kelnemaen. Selnemaen. Mbaen.`  
> `Zev. Fahohm. Mira. Daku. Ravohm. Kavkrez. Fahreth.`

The set alternates firm closures (`Nem`, `Zev`, `Kavkrez`, `Fahreth`) with open-vowel releases (`Mira`, `Daku`, the `-aen` verbs). `Mbaen` retains Celan's marked initial nasal-plus-stop articulation rather than hiding the inherited `Mb-` family.

No exact headword collisions were found for the proposed forms in the current source lexicon, approved expansion layer, root inventory, or expressions layer.

The four proposed primitives are no longer than two syllables:

- `Nem` — one syllable
- `Zev` — one syllable
- `Mira` — two syllables
- `Daku` — two syllables

## Matters intentionally deferred

- a general standalone headword for *need*
- separate general verbs for *accept* and *refuse* beyond existing `Lia`, `Ka`, `Ver`, and established constructions
- guilt separated from shame; this requires its own review of `Veth'tharaen`
- neutral physical solitude or being alone
- clinical anxiety disorders
- automatic adjective, person-label, or abstract-noun families derived from the proposed roots
- national origins or characteristic national uses

All candidates are proposed as universal Celan. National usage should be added only if later cultural evidence shows that a particular form or application is characteristically associated with one nation.

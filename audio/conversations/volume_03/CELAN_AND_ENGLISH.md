# Everyday conversations — Volume 3: precision in everyday life

Thirty standalone conversations about the amount, object, measure, or place that actually matters. Savi tends the shared supplies; Dalen builds and repairs; Meva carries goods between places. The exchanges grow from the [English-first draft](../../../learning_center/everyday_conversations_volume_03_english_first_draft.md), with turns reshaped where Celan says the same useful thing more naturally. Actions in italics are silent. The Celan column is learner spelling, not a pronunciation script. Written **I** is pronounced **ee**.

## 1. The fifth flask

*Dalen sets the water by Meva's things. Savi finds another flask in the cupboard.*

| Speaker | Celan | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dalen | `Quen drenkavvekin. Ilinor.` | Four flasks. That's all. |
| Meva | `Ra rinaen I-ka drenkavvek lo?` | Where is my flask? |
| Dalen | `Em. Ra rinaen an Ya?` | Um. Is it with you? |
| Savi | `An xarvek. Rinaen deni zun.` | In the cupboard. This one is empty. |
| Dalen | `Lia'ser. Nor-dor I dren an deni.` | Right. I'll put water in this one. |
| Meva | `Peth drenkavvekin. Ra rinaen dren worlian?` | Five flasks. Is there enough water? |
| Savi | `Lia. Va drenaen Ya shalnor min Ya.` | Yes. Drink before you go. |

# Week 6, Lesson B: Movement or residence

**Objective:** Distinguish going somewhere from staying there.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** The Architecture of Relation: Prepositions, Place, and Time, Varan and the universal continuous suffix. **Focus forms:** `Var`, `Varan`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `var kadron an morl.` | The man goes to the mountain. |
| `varan I an Varthas.` | I am residing in the city. |

Var describes going; Varan is the established word for residence or location.

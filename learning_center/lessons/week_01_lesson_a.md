# Week 1, Lesson A: Hear the action

**Objective:** Identify the action before its participants.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** The Revised Celan Phonetic Guide, Action-First Sentence Structure. **Focus forms:** `Var`, `I`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `var I dren.` | I go to the water. |
| `ohmaen I Ya.` | I love or cherish you. |

Both clauses lead with the verb. The pronoun I follows it and sounds like ee.

## Practice

### Build Words

1. What does `Var` mean in this lesson?
2. Write the Celan form for **I or me, pronounced ee**.

### Read, build, and fix meaning

3. Read model A. What does it mean in English?
4. Read model B. What does it mean in English?
5. In model A, label the action, speaker, and destination.
6. How is the written pronoun I pronounced?

## Independent task

Point to the action in each line before translating. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Listen for the first word; it names the action.

## Answer key and feedback

1. **go or move.**
2. **`I`.**
3. **I go to the water.**
4. **I love or cherish you.**
5. **var / I / dren**
6. **ee**

# Week 4, Lesson A: Roots as seeds

**Objective:** Trace a recorded water word without inventing a suffix.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Derivation and Word Formation. **Focus forms:** `Dren`, `Drenaen`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Dren` | water |
| `Drenaen` | to drink or take liquid |

Drenaen is an approved headword; the verb-forming ending is -aen.

## Practice

### Build Words

1. What does `Dren` mean in this lesson?
2. Write the Celan form for **to drink or take liquid**.

### Read, build, and fix meaning

3. Read model A. What does it mean in English?
4. Read model B. What does it mean in English?
5. Which model is the water noun?
6. Which is the approved verb meaning to drink?

## Independent task

Explain the relation between water and drinking. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Check the recorded word before guessing from a pattern.

# Week 12, Lesson C: Capstone: live in the language

**Objective:** Combine known form, meaning, and social choice in a bounded task.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Action-First Sentence Structure, Questions and Commands, Capitalization and Emphasis. **Focus forms:** `Var`, `Ohmaen`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `var I an dren.` | I go to the water. |
| `ohmaen I Ya.` | I love or cherish you. |

Both clauses begin with an action, preserve meaningful capitals, and can sit inside a longer scene.

## Independent task

Write a six-to-eight-line Celan exchange or description using at least four taught patterns: an action-first clause, a question or command, a time or negation marker, and a relation or possession form. Annotate each line with the rule or model you used.

## Hint

Use the taught forms and label each new line with the model you adapted.

Models A and B are safe starting lines, not a complete sample response.

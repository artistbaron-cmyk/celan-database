# Week 11, Lesson B: The friend who goes

**Objective:** Recognize Lior after the noun it describes.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Relative Clauses. **Focus forms:** `Lior`, `Ser`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Ser Lior var an dren.` | The friend who goes to the water. |
| `var I an terra ser Ser Lior var an dren.` | I go home with the friend who goes to the water. |

Lior introduces information about Ser; it does not begin a new independent question.

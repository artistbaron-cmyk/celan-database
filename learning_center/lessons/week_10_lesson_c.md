# Week 10, Lesson C: Read a short exchange

**Objective:** Read known sentence shapes without translating word by word.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Action-First Sentence Structure, Questions and Commands, Capitalization and Emphasis. **Focus forms:** `Ra`, `Ser`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `var I an dren.` | I go to the water. |
| `ra var ser an dren?` | Does the friend go to the water? |

The first line states an action; the second asks about another participant.

## Practice

### Build Words

1. What does `Ra` mean in this lesson?
2. Write the Celan form for **friend**.

### Read, build, and fix meaning

3. Read model A. What does it mean in English?
4. Read model B. What does it mean in English?
5. Which model is a question?
6. Who is the subject of that question?

## Independent task

Read the two-line exchange and answer who goes where. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Mark the opening signpost before translating.

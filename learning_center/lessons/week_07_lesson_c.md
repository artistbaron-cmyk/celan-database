# Week 7, Lesson C: Groups and third persons

**Objective:** Read possessor-ka before the possessed item.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Possession: spacing and suffix hierarchy. **Focus forms:** `Ilin`, `La`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Ilin-ka emil` | our meal |
| `La-ka kelvor` | their mouth |

The possessor carries -ka; the following noun is what is possessed.

## Practice

### Build Words

1. What does `Ilin` mean in this lesson?
2. Write the Celan form for **he, she, or singular they**.

### Read, build, and fix meaning

3. Read model A. What does it mean in English?
4. Read model B. What does it mean in English?
5. Who possesses emil in A?
6. Who possesses kelvor in B?

## Independent task

Identify the owner and item in each phrase. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Ask which word names the owner.

## Answer key and feedback

1. **we.**
2. **`La`.**
3. **our meal**
4. **their mouth**
5. **Ilin, the speaker's group**
6. **La, a third-person singular referent**

**Independent response guide:** Ilin is the possessor of emil; La is the possessor of kelvor.

# Week 12, Lesson B: Rehearse an exchange

**Objective:** Choose lines for care and team coordination.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Cultural Voice: How Ohnosha Speaks Celan Differently. **Focus forms:** `Dro`, `Belnoraen`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Dro?` | Water?; a gentle offer of care. |
| `Ra belnoraen Ilin?` | Are we synchronized or ready together? |

The same learner can speak gently to one person and check readiness with a group.

## Practice

### Build Words

1. What does `Dro` mean in this lesson?
2. Write the Celan form for **synchronize**.

### Read, build, and fix meaning

3. Read model A. What does it mean in English?
4. Read model B. What does it mean in English?
5. Which line suits a person who may need quiet help?
6. Which line checks a group's readiness?

## Independent task

Open a brief scene with care, then check group readiness. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Choose a line by audience and purpose.

**Independent response guide:** Use Dro? for individual care, then Ra belnoraen Ilin? for group readiness.

# Week 10, Lesson A: Sentence openings and I

**Objective:** Keep ordinary sentence openings lowercase while capitalizing I.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Capitalization and Emphasis. **Focus forms:** `I`, `Var`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `var I an dren.` | I go to the water. |
| `ohmaen I Ya.` | I love or cherish you. |

The first ordinary verb is lowercase; the pronoun I remains capitalized.

## Independent task

Edit two ordinary sentences for Celan capitals. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Capitalization follows meaning and named exceptions, not English sentence starts.

3. **I go to the water.**
4. **I love or cherish you.**
5. **var I an dren.**

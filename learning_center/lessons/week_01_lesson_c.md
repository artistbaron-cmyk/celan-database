# Week 1, Lesson C: First Motion scene

**Objective:** Choose a command and a gentle offer in context.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Questions and Commands. **Focus forms:** `Dro`, `Var`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `va var an dren!` | Go to the water! |
| `Dro?` | Water?; a gentle offer of care. |

The first line directs action; the second offers care without requiring explanation.

## Practice

### Build Words

1. What does `Dro` mean in this lesson?
2. Write the Celan form for **go or move**.

### Read, build, and fix meaning

3. Read model A. What does it mean in English?
4. Read model B. What does it mean in English?
5. Which model directs movement?
6. Which model offers help without demanding an explanation?

## Independent task

At a doorway, give a direction; afterward, offer water. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Decide whether the person needs direction or an offer.

## Answer key and feedback

1. **water.**
2. **`Var`.**
3. **Go to the water!**
4. **Water?; a gentle offer of care.**
5. **A**
6. **B**

**Independent response guide:** Use model A for the direction, then model B for the offer.

**Review tomorrow:** Revisit the two headwords, then reproduce model B without looking. Review it again after three and seven days if correct.

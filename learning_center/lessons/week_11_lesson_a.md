# Week 11, Lesson A: If a friend goes

**Objective:** Recognize rath introducing an if-clause.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Conditionals: future and past. **Focus forms:** `Rath`, `Ser`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `rath var ser, var I dren.` | If the friend goes, I go to the water. |
| `rath nor-var ser, nor-var I dren.` | If the friend will go, I will go to the water. |

Rath opens the condition; both clauses keep an action before their participants.

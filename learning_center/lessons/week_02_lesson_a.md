# Week 2, Lesson A: Speaker and listener

**Objective:** Recognize I and Ya in a short VSO clause.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Personal Pronouns, Action-First Sentence Structure. **Focus forms:** `I`, `Ya`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `var I dren.` | I go to the water. |
| `ohmaen I Ya.` | I love or cherish you. |

I is the speaker; Ya is the listener. The verb still comes first.

## Practice

### Build Words

1. What does `I` mean in this lesson?
2. Write the Celan form for **you, one listener**.

### Read, build, and fix meaning

3. Read model A. What does it mean in English?
4. Read model B. What does it mean in English?
5. Who speaks in both models?
6. Who is addressed in model B?

## Independent task

Identify speaker and listener in each model. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Ask who is speaking and who is addressed.

## Answer key and feedback

3. **I go to the water.**
4. **I love or cherish you.**
5. **I**

**Independent response guide:** In A, I is the speaker. In B, I is the speaker and Ya is the listener.

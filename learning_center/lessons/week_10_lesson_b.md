# Week 10, Lesson B: Meaningful capitals

**Objective:** Distinguish mundane ground from archetypal Earth in the guide.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Capitalization and Emphasis, Capitalization examples. **Focus forms:** `Terra`, `I`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `terra` | ordinary earth, soil, or ground |
| `Terra` | archetypal Earth or the Heart of Terra |

Capital T signals archetypal force in this teaching contrast.

## Practice

### Build Words

1. What does `Terra` mean in this lesson?
2. Write the Celan form for **I or me, always capitalized**.

### Read, build, and fix meaning

3. Read model A. What does it mean in English?
4. Read model B. What does it mean in English?
5. Which model refers to ordinary soil?
6. Which model treats Earth as archetypal?

## Independent task

Explain why a writer chose Terra instead of terra. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Do not capitalize a word merely because it starts a sentence.

## Answer key and feedback

1. **Earth or ground, with meaning shaped by context and capitalization.**
2. **`I`.**
3. **ordinary earth, soil, or ground**
4. **archetypal Earth or the Heart of Terra**
5. **A: terra**
6. **B: Terra**

**Independent response guide:** Terra signals archetypal Earth in this contrast; terra means ordinary ground.

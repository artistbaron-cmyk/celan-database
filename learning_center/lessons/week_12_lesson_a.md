# Week 12, Lesson A: Repair and retrieve

**Objective:** Retrieve question, negation, and possession patterns together.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Questions and Commands, Possession: spacing and suffix hierarchy. **Focus forms:** `Ver`, `Ilin`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `ra ver var ser an dren?` | Does the friend not go to the water? |
| `Ilin-ka emil` | our meal |

The first uses ra plus ver before the verb; the second attaches -ka to the possessor.

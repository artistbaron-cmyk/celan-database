# Week 6, Lesson A: Mark a place

**Objective:** Recognize an as a physical place relation.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** The Architecture of Relation: Prepositions, Place, and Time, Action-First Sentence Structure. **Focus forms:** `An`, `Dren`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `var I dren.` | I go to the water. |
| `var I an dren.` | I go to the water, with an explicit destination. |

The second line states the destination with an; the bare goal is also attested with var.

## Answer key and feedback

2. **`Dren`.**
3. **I go to the water.**
4. **I go to the water, with an explicit destination.**
5. **B**
6. **No**

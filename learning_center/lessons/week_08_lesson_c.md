# Week 8, Lesson C: Count in an exchange

**Objective:** Distinguish a built quantity from subtraction.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Numbers and Basic Math. **Focus forms:** `Ven`, `Ten-del`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Ten-del` | twenty |
| `Hek-quen Ven Hek-del` | four hundred minus two hundred, yielding two hundred |

Ven marks subtraction; a built number without Ven expresses a quantity.

## Practice

## Independent task

Tell whether a market note names a quantity or a subtraction. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

Look for Ven before calculating.

2. **`Ten-del`.**
3. **twenty**
4. **four hundred minus two hundred, yielding two hundred**

**Independent response guide:** A is the quantity twenty. B is subtraction: four hundred minus two hundred.

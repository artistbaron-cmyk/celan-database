**Grammar topics:** Cultural Voice: How Ohnosha Speaks Celan Differently. **Focus forms:** `Belnoraen`, `Mura`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Ra belnoraen Ilin?` | Are we synchronized or ready together? |
| `Kel La ser mura.` | They speak with smoke; they evade a usable answer. |

One is a Mechuman coordination check; the other can accuse someone of evasion.

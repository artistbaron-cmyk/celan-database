# Week 3, Lesson A: Say no to an action

**Objective:** Place ver directly before the verb for a negative statement.

**Source links:** [Grammar Guide](../../dictionary/app_data/grammar_guide.csv) · [Dictionary headwords](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_entries.csv) · [Dictionary examples](../../dictionary/app_data/dictionary_examples.csv) · [Expressions](../../dictionary/app_data/expressions_app.json).  
**Grammar topics:** Negation. **Focus forms:** `Ver`, `Var`.

**Suggested session:** 3 minutes with models, 10 minutes practice, 8 minutes independent use, then a brief review.

## Model and explanation

| Celan | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `var I dren.` | I go to the water. |
| `ver var I dren.` | I do not go to the water. |

Ver before var negates the action.

## Practice

### Build Words

### Read, build, and fix meaning

3. Read model A. What does it mean in English?

## Independent task

Tell a companion that you are not going to the water. Use the two models as choices or frames; answer without looking.

## Hint

The negative sign sits immediately before the action.

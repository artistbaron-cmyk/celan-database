# Everyday conversations — Volume 2, Celan vernacular pass

These scenes keep the everyday situations of the English-first draft while letting established Celan choose the actual lines. Each English turn gives a natural translation that a learner can infer from the Celan and the scene. English may supply ordinary words such as “it” or “the” when Celan leaves them to context; it does not add a different action, person, object, time, or negation. Short fragments are intentional speech, not missing textbook sentences. The written Celan is for learners; mood and pronunciation cues belong in separate recording copies.

# Volume 2 — mood-matched ElevenLabs copies

These are paste-ready performance copies of the [written Volume 2 conversations](everyday_conversations_volume_02.md). They keep the same speakers and Celan words. For the recordings, **I** becomes **ee**, written **x** becomes **ks** for the chosen reading, commas and periods are removed, and question marks remain. The bracketed cues tell the voices how to play a line; they are not Celan words and do not belong in subtitles. These sound spellings never replace the written Celan forms.

Scene 1 is the listening reference. Scenes 2–30 follow its approach: one cue for each turn, with a second cue only where the speaker changes tone inside that turn. Play each scene as a conversation, giving the other voice room to land before replying. Scene 30 needs a generous pause after Toren's **lia** before the goodnights.

**Pronunciation check before recording:** Compare unfamiliar words with their established audio. Keep **ee** for written **I** and the chosen **ks** reading for written **x** in the upload copy. Do not apply a blanket change to **ae** or **-aen**; those sounds have accepted alternatives, and individual headword trials have their own chosen takes. If a word sounds wrong, adjust that line's recording input and audition it again.

Scenes 2–4 have demo takes in ElevenLabs History. For Scene 4, use the newer take beginning **`[shivering] rivaen ee an ksarphelor`**; earlier takes used the unadjusted **x** spelling in the speech input.

# Volume 3 — ElevenLabs dialogue performance copy

Derived from [the written Celan and English scenes](everyday_conversations_volume_03_celan_vernacular.md).
Savi: Lily. Dalen: Ayinde. Meva: Ehshara. Use Eleven v4 Dialogue mode.
Tags and sound spellings are recording instructions only. Subtitles use the written Celan.
Periods and commas are omitted so the acting can flow; question marks remain.

## 1. The fifth flask

```text
Dalen: [certain] Quen drehn-kahv-veh-keen Ilinor
Meva: [checking] Ra rinaen ee-ka drehn-kahv-vehk lo?
Dalen: [sheepish] Em Ra rinaen an Ya?
Savi: [helpful] An ksarvek Rinaen deni zun
Dalen: [apologetic] Lia'ser Nor-dor ee dren an deni
Meva: [checking] Peth drehn-kahv-veh-keen Ra rinaen dren worlian?
Savi: [warm] Lia Va drenaen Ya shalnor min Ya
```

## 2. Leave this one empty

```text
Savi: [puzzled] Rinaen ksarvek deni zun Ra kavelzhiraen Ya morlak?
Dalen: [explaining] Ka Nor-dor Meva saren an deni
Savi: [checking] Ra dor ee morlak an ksarvek denya?
Dalen: [clear] Lia Va ver dor Ya morlak an deni
Savi: [understanding] Lia'ser Nor-varan deni zun
```

## 3. One too many

```text
Meva: [firm] Rinaen senar karmekin an deni Tha-aen eenko peth
Dalen: [sheepish] Tha-dor ee kareth deni an deni
Meva: [testing] Ra vekaen Ya kareth?
Dalen: [agreeable] Lia'ser Va talaen Ya peth karmekin
Meva: [dry] Lia Rinaen ee-ka korinin woreth an nor-ka
```

## 4. One for each

```text
Savi: [counting] Tren drehn-kahv-veh-keen Lia'ser
Dalen: [noticing] Rinaen quen serin an deni
Savi: [sheepish] Em Tha-shen ee drehn-kahv-veh-keen Ver tha-shen ee serin
Dalen: [practical] Ekor ser unar drehn-kahv-vehk
Savi: [concerned] Rinaen unar ser ka dren
Dalen: [offering] Rinaen ee-ka drehn-kahv-vehk an ksarvek
Savi: [relieved] Va shan Ya deni an ee Nor-dor ee dren an deni
```

## 5. The other two coins

```text
Dalen: [concerned] Tha-aen Ya peth shena Rinaen tren an pralor
Savi: [matter of fact] An ksarvek vethvar Ya
Dalen: [defensive] Tha-shalaen ee ksarvek Ver tha-shalaen ee shena
Savi: [dry] Va kaksaraen Ya deni
Dalen: [realizing] Lia'ser Peth shena
```

## 6. Water for the people staying

```text
Savi: [protective] Va dar Ya Rinaen felin dren an deni
Meva: [honest] Rinaen ee drorav Ver talaen ee ilinor dren
Savi: [helpful] Rinaen droshen an ksarvek Va talaen Ya unar droshen
Meva: [checking] Ra rinaen dren worlian?
Savi: [reassuring] Lia Va drenaen Ya
```

## 7. Half the water

```text
Dalen: [confident] Tal ee unar drenvor an Ya Tal ee unar an Meva
Savi: [correcting] Rinaen deni feleth Rinaen denya woreth
Dalen: [defensive] Tha-aen Ya unar thal del
Savi: [precise] Dren Unar thal del
Dalen: [understanding] Lia'ser Nor-shen ee dren
```

## 8. Keep a quarter

```text
Meva: [checking] Ra vekaen ee ilinor morlak?
Savi: [firm] Ka Unar thal quen
Meva: [curious] Ra dor Ya morlak an ksarvek zhe?
Savi: [explaining] Rinaen felin morlak an deni Nor-rinaen serin emilrav nor kilveth
Meva: [cooperative] Lia'ser Va dor Ya morlak deni an ksarvek Ver vekaen ee deni
Savi: [appreciative] Lia Va talaen Ya morlak denya
```

## 9. Five of eight

```text
Savi: [planning] Rinaen oven shen morlak Peth thal oven
Meva: [checking] Tha-shen ee quen shen an deni Ra rinaen unar shen lo?
Dalen: [focused] An pralor Shen ee deni an nor-ka
Meva: [impatient] Ra dar ee nor aenor?
Dalen: [measured] Lia Va dar Ya
Savi: [urgent] Meva nor-min an aenor
Dalen: [hurrying] Lia'ser Va talaen Ya unar shen
Meva: [relieved] Peth shen Lia Nor-min ee
```

## 10. Enough for whom?

```text
Dalen: [certain] Rinaen morlak worlian
Savi: [noticing] Rinaen del serin an thalor
Dalen: [surprised] lee-ahlohr'ka? Ra rinaen Lalin emilrav?
Savi: [concerned] Lia Rinaen felin morlak an deni
Dalen: [generous] Lia'ser Nor-talaen ee ee-ka morlak
```

## 11. The wrong unit


## 12. From the door

```text
Meva: [checking] An deni?
Dalen: [precise] Ka Unar shar thal vorkor
Meva: [puzzled] Tha-var Ya Ra rinaen shalkor deni neh-voo thal vorkor?
Dalen: [confirming] Lia Va dor Ya shalkor an deni
Meva: [dry] Lia'ser Va dar Ya an vorkor
```

## 13. Which five?

```text
Savi: [clear] Peth shen morlak Meva
Meva: [mishearing] Peth shena?
Savi: [correcting] Ka Shen morlak Rinaen shena an ksarvek
Meva: [sheepish] Lia'ser Talaen ee shena felath
Savi: [helpful] Lia Va talaen Ya peth shen morlak
```

## 14. Too heavy

## 15. Start at the door

## 20. Outside the door


## 22. Between the chests

```text
Savi: [checking] Ra tha-talaen Ya karvek?
Meva: [answering] Ka Rinaen karvek serdel ksarvek deni ser ksarvek denya
Savi: [explaining] Tha-shalaen ee ksarvekin Ver tha-shalaen ee karvek

```

## 23. The closed way

```text
Savi: [checking] Ra tha-min Ya an theren?
Meva: [frustrated] Ka Ver kaksaraen vorkor an mekshara
Dalen: [suggesting] Ra min Ya shan morlshan?
Meva: [firm] Rinaen morlshan feleth Ver min ee shan morlshan ser karvek
Savi: [guiding] Va min Ya an mekshara denla
Meva: [tired] Lia'ser Va dar Ya nor aenor Rinaen ee felkal
```

## 24. Leave the boots outside

```text
Savi: [firm] Rinaen Ya-ka trakorin gormeth Va dor Ya deni thalor

Savi: [practical] Va shan Ya kelpral an ee Va dar Ya thalor
Meva: [complying] Lia'ser Va talaen Ya kelpral Nor-dor ee trakorin thalor
Savi: [welcoming] Vara va anshanaen Ya
```

## 25. The nearer stop

```text
Meva: [decided] Nor-min ee an theren shalnor evan

Meva: [appreciative] Elan Nor-min ee an evan vethnor theren
```

## 26. How far from here?

```text
Dalen: [asking] Ra rinaen theren ah-reen thal deni sha?

Meva: [checking] Ra shalaen Ya mekshara?
Dalen: [confirming] Lia Rinaen an sora
```

## 27. Close, but a long walk

## 28. At least five

```text
Savi: [counting] mbeh-too peth drehn-kahv-veh-keen Rinaen quen an deni
Meva: [concerned] fehl-dreh-nah-ehn dren thal drehn-kahv-vehk denya
Dalen: [reassuring] Lia'ser Nor-zhaelpralaen ee drehn-kahv-vehk
Meva: [urgent] Ra zhaelpralaen Ya drehn-kahv-vehk shalnor min ee?
Dalen: [concentrating] Lia Va dar Ya nor aenor
Savi: [checking] Ra fehl-dreh-nah-ehn dren thal drehn-kahv-vehk an nor-ka?
Dalen: [relieved] Ka Ver fehl-dreh-nah-ehn dren Va shan Ya drehn-kahv-vehk an Meva
Meva: [ready] Peth drehn-kahv-veh-keen Nor-min ee
```

## 29. No more than two

```text

Dalen: [offering] Lia Nor-vekaen ee unar ksarvek Nor-shalaen ee shalkor an theren
Meva: [agreeing] Lia'ser Nor-vekaen ee del
Savi: [checking] Lia Va dar Ya Nor-shen ee ksarvekin wek'aen
Meva: [dry] Tren Va ver dor Ya ksarvek denla an ee
```

# Week 1: The First Motion

Phase 1: Entering Ohnosha

## Week Purpose
Week 1 teaches the learner how Celan moves. The goal is not to explain everything at once, but to get the learner speaking immediately through action-first sentence order, clear pronunciation, and simple commands.

## Learner Outcome
By the end of the week, the learner can:
- recognize Celan as a VSO language
- pronounce short Celan phrases clearly
- produce basic imperatives
- complete a short command-based mission without help

## Core Idea
In Celan, action comes first. That makes the language feel immediate, active, and alive. For beginners, that means the first lesson should not be a grammar lecture. It should feel like stepping into motion.

## Weekly Theme
Action carries intention.

## Canon Anchors
- VSO sentence order
- Clear pronunciation and flow
- Imperatives formed with `Va`
- Example forms:
  - `var I dren.`
  - `Va var dren!`

## Lesson 1: Hear the Motion

### Goal
Help the learner hear that Celan starts with the verb, not the subject.

### Teach
- Celan primarily follows Verb-Subject-Object order.
- The first word of a sentence is often the action.
- Each syllable should be pronounced clearly and smoothly.

### Try
- Listen to 5 short model sentences.
- Tap the verb first, subject second, object third.
- Repeat each sentence aloud twice.

### Exercise
1. Mark the verb in each sentence.
2. Rearrange mixed word cards into the correct order.
3. Say the sentence out loud after putting it in order.

### Fun Layer
Call this the "Motion Check." The learner is not analyzing grammar yet. They are learning to recognize how Celan starts moving.

### Checkpoint
The learner can identify the verb in 5 simple Celan sentences and place the parts in VSO order.

## Lesson 2: Give the Command

### Goal
Teach imperatives as the fastest way to speak a full sentence.

### Teach
- Imperatives begin with `Va`.
- Commands are direct, useful, and easy to practice on day one.
- Commands are one of the cleanest ways to experience Celan as an action language.

### Try
- Read and repeat 5 command forms.
- Start with short, useful actions.
- Focus on confident delivery, not perfection.

### Exercise
1. Turn English commands into Celan-style commands.
2. Match a command to the right scene.
3. Speak the command with the correct energy.

### Example Practice
- `Va var dren!`
- `Va var!`
- `Va kel!`

### Fun Layer
Call this the "Gatekeeper Mission." The learner must use commands to move through a short scene, as if the language is opening a door.

### Checkpoint
The learner can produce 5 basic imperatives using `Va` without prompts.

## Lesson 3: Speak the First Mission

### Goal
Combine VSO order and imperatives in a short speaking loop.

### Teach
- The learner now uses action-first speech in context.
- Short commands become a tiny survival language.
- Confidence matters more than complexity.

### Try
- Hear a 3-step mini scene.
- Repeat each line.
- Respond with a command or simple action sentence.

### Exercise
1. Listen to a short situation.
2. Choose the right Celan response.
3. Speak your answer aloud.

### Scene Ideas
- You are at a doorway.
- You are being asked to move.
- You are guiding someone to water.

### Fun Layer
Call this the "First Motion Trial." The learner is proving they can enter the language world by acting inside it.

### Checkpoint
The learner can complete a short 3-line command scene using VSO awareness and at least one imperatively formed sentence.

## Weekly Review

### Review Focus
- VSO order
- clear pronunciation
- imperatives with `Va`
- action-first thinking

### Review Activity
Use a quick four-part replay:
1. Hear it
2. Tap it
3. Say it
4. Use it

### Review Question
- What comes first in Celan sentences?
- How do commands begin?

## Week 1 Unlock
The learner unlocks the right to speak in motion. In the learning center, this can appear as:
- a first badge
- a new scene
- a short status line such as "You can move in Celan now"

## Notes for the Learning Center
- Keep the UI simple and immediate.
- Avoid long explanations on the first pass.
- Use large action buttons and short audio prompts.
- Make every exercise feel like a small success.

## End-of-Week Mastery Check
The learner passes Week 1 if they can:
- explain that Celan starts with the verb
- say 5 short VSO examples correctly
- use `Va` to form 5 basic commands
- complete one short mission without help

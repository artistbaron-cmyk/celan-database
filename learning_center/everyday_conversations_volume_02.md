# Celan everyday conversations — Volume 2: Plans change

These 30 short scenes follow **Nara** and **Toren** through an ordinary stretch of life: a plan, a forgotten tool, weather, shopping, fatigue, and a repair at home. **Varadan** is the market seller. The Celan blocks are the written scripts. Punctuation may be adjusted in a separate ElevenLabs upload copy without changing the dialogue. Each English segment follows one speaker turn and translates what that turn actually says. A speaker may answer with a word or a fragment; the actors should let the other person's line matter before speaking.

Read the written pronoun **I** as **ee**. Nara and Toren are character names, not dictionary headwords. These conversations are newly composed from established Celan words and grammar.

The [mood-matched ElevenLabs copies](elevenlabs_upload_volume_02_mood_index.md) are the recording versions of all 30 scenes. Keep this document as the written script.

## Part I — The plan

### 1. Before leaving

**Situation:** Morning at Nara's door. Toren is ready to go.

```text
Nara: Nekvar, Toren.
Toren: Nekvar. Ra var Ya an evan?
Nara: Lia. Nor-var I an evan nor aenor.
Toren: Var I sera an evan.
Nara: Dar I an vorkor.
Toren: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** Good morning, Toren / Good morning. Are you going to the market? / Yes. I'll go to the market in a moment. / I'm going to the market too. / I'll wait at the door. / Okay.

### 2. The toolkit

**Situation:** Toren reaches the door and realizes something is missing.

```text
Toren: Em. Kavelzhiraen I karvek.
Nara: Rakel?
Toren: Ka karvek. Rinaen karvek an teremil.
Nara: Dar I an vorkor.
Toren: Nor-var I an teremil.
Nara: Va var Ya an deni nor aenor.
Toren: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** Um. I forgot the toolkit. / Pardon? / No toolkit. The toolkit is at home. / I'll wait at the door. / I'll go home. / Come here in a moment. / Okay.

### 3. Rain

**Situation:** By the time Toren returns, rain has started.

```text
Nara: Kes I drenalor an esh.
Toren: Rinaen eshnor veleth.
Nara: Ra var Ya an evan nethnor?
Toren: Ka. Ver var I an evan.
Nara: Ra rinaen morlak an teremil?
Toren: Ka. Ver rinaen morlak an teremil.
Nara: Nor-var I an Eshvan.
Toren: Var I ser Ya.
```

**English:** I see rain in the sky. / The weather is cold. / Are you still going to the market? / No. I'm not going to the market. / Is there bread at home? / No. There isn't bread at home. / I'll go to the shop. / I'm going with you.

### 4. Under the shelter

**Situation:** They pause under shelter before the shop.

```text
Toren: Rivaen I an xarphelor.
Nara: Ra rinaen Ya felkal?
Toren: Ka. Rinaen I drorav.
Nara: Rinaen dren an drenkavvek.
Toren: Ra rinaen dren worlian?
Nara: Lia. Rinaen dren worlian.
Toren: Drenaen I dren.
```

**English:** I'm shivering in the shelter. / Are you tired? / No. I'm thirsty. / There is water in the flask. / Is there enough water? / Yes. There is enough water. / I drink water.

### 5. Left, not right

**Situation:** Nara gives directions while Toren leads.

```text
Nara: Va jekaen Ya naku.
Toren: Sora?
Nara: Ka. Naku.
Toren: Rakel. Naku.
Nara: Lia. Rinaen Eshvan an naku.
Toren: Kes I Eshvan.
```

**English:** Turn left. / Right? / No. Left. / Pardon. Left. / Yes. The shop is on the left. / I see the shop.

### 6. Back at the door

**Situation:** They return with bread. Nara remembers the morning's delay.

```text
Nara: Tha-anvanesh Ilin morlak.
Toren: Lia. Rinaen I emilrav.
Nara: Ra velzhiraen Ya karvek?
Toren: Lia. Velzhiraen I karvek.
Nara: Lia'ser.
Toren: Ra dovkelaen Ya I?
Nara: Lia.
```

**English:** We bought bread. / Yes. I'm hungry. / Do you remember the toolkit? / Yes. I remember the toolkit. / Okay. / Are you teasing me? / Yes.

## Part II — At home

### 7. The missing bowl

**Situation:** They begin making lunch, but the bowl is not where it usually is.

```text
Toren: Ra rinaen drenvor an pralor?
Nara: Ka.
Toren: Rakel? Kes I drenvor an xaror.
Nara: Tal I drenvor.
Toren: Va dor Ya drenvor an pralor.
Nara: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** Is the bowl on the table? / No. / Pardon? I see the bowl in the room. / I take the bowl. / Put the bowl on the table. / Okay.

### 8. A hand with the plates

**Situation:** Toren is setting out food while Nara carries the plates.

```text
Toren: Ra mbaen I Ya?
Nara: Lia. Va tal Ya emilpral.
Toren: Tal I emilpral.
Nara: Dor I morlak an emilpral.
Toren: Ra rinaen dren an drenkavvek?
Nara: Lia. Rinaen dren worlian.
```

**English:** Can I help you? / Yes. Take the plate. / I take the plate. / I put the bread on the plate. / Is there water in the flask? / Yes. There is enough water.

### 9. The chair

**Situation:** Toren notices that the chair needs work.

```text
Toren: Kes I rethpral an nethor.
Nara: Rakel?
Toren: Nethor. Kes I rethpral.
Nara: Ra mbaen I Ya?
Toren: Lia. Va tal Ya karvek.
Nara: Tal I karvek.
```

**English:** I see a mistake in the chair. / Pardon? / The chair. I see a mistake. / Can I help you? / Yes. Take the toolkit. / I take the toolkit.

### 10. Turn it over

**Situation:** They examine the chair together.

```text
Nara: Va jekaen Ya nethor.
Toren: Jekaen I nethor.
Nara: Kes I rethpral.
Toren: Ra zhaelpralaen Ya nethor?
Nara: Lia. Zhaelpralaen I nethor.
Toren: Dar I ser Ya.
```

**English:** Turn the chair. / I turn the chair. / I see the mistake. / Will you repair the chair? / Yes. I repair the chair. / I'll wait with you.

### 11. Not the way I meant

**Situation:** Toren reaches for the toolkit when Nara meant the chair.

```text
Nara: Tha-rethnemaen Ya I.
Toren: Rakel?
Nara: Va jekaen Ya nethor. Ver tal Ya karvek.
Toren: Lia'ser. Jekaen I nethor.
Nara: Lia. Zhaelpralaen I nethor.
Toren: Dar I.
```

**English:** You misunderstood me. / Pardon? / Turn the chair. Don't take the toolkit. / Okay. I turn the chair. / Yes. I repair the chair. / I'll wait.

### 12. Lunch can wait

**Situation:** Lunch is on the table, but the repair is nearly done.

```text
Toren: Rinaen I emilrav.
Nara: Rinaen morlak an pralor.
Toren: Ra zhaelpralaen Ya nethor nethnor?
Nara: Lia. Felath.
Toren: Dar I.
Nara: Lia'ser. Velaen Ilin morlak vethnor.
```

**English:** I'm hungry. / The bread is on the table. / Are you still repairing the chair? / Yes. Almost. / I'll wait. / Okay. We'll eat bread afterward.

## Part III — The next errand

### 13. No bread today

**Situation:** The following day, Nara asks Varadan for bread.

```text
Nara: Zhilva, Varadan.
Varadan: Zhilva.
Nara: Ra rinaen morlak an van?
Varadan: Ka. Ver rinaen morlak an van.
Nara: Rakel?
Varadan: Rinaen vilor zhaeleth.
Nara: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** Hello, Varadan. / Hello. / Is there bread at the shop? / No. There isn't bread at the shop. / Pardon? / The fruit is fresh. / I see.

### 14. Fruit instead

**Situation:** Nara tells Toren what the seller has.

```text
Toren: Ra tha-anvanesh Ya morlak?
Nara: Ka. Ver rinaen morlak an van.
Toren: Rinaen I emilrav.
Nara: Rinaen vilor zhaeleth.
Toren: Tharsharaen I vilor.
Nara: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** Did you buy bread? / No. There isn't bread at the shop. / I'm hungry. / The fruit is fresh. / I choose fruit. / Okay.

### 15. Two coins?

**Situation:** Toren asks Varadan the price before buying.

```text
Toren: Ra aen Ya belshen vilor?
Varadan: Del shena.
Toren: Mmm. Del shena?
Varadan: Lia.
Toren: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** Do you say the price of the fruit? / Two coins. / Hmm. Two coins? / Yes. / Okay.

### 16. No coins

**Situation:** Toren reaches for the money and realizes it was left at home.

```text
Toren: Ka shena.
Nara: Rakel?
Toren: Kavelzhiraen I shena.
Nara: Nor-var I an teremil.
Toren: Dar I an evan.
Nara: Zharinor.
```

**English:** No coins. / Pardon? / I forgot the coins. / I'll go home. / I'll wait at the market. / See you soon.

### 17. Back with the money

**Situation:** Nara returns to the stall.

```text
Nara: Vekaen I shena var Ya.
Toren: Lia'ser.
Nara: Anvanesh I vilor.
Varadan: Tal I vilor an Ya.
Toren: Rinaen I emilrav.
Nara: Nor-var Ilin an teremil.
```

**English:** I bring the coins to you. / Okay. / I buy fruit. / I give you fruit. / I'm hungry. / We'll go home.

### 18. Fruit on the plate

**Situation:** They finally eat at home.

```text
Nara: Dor I vilor an emilpral.
Toren: Rinaen vilor zhaeleth.
Nara: Rinaen I emilrav.
Toren: Velaen Ilin vilor.
Nara: Rinaen dren an drenkavvek.
Toren: Lia. Drenaen I dren.
```

**English:** I put fruit on the plate. / The fruit is fresh. / I'm hungry. / We eat fruit. / There is water in the flask. / Yes. I drink water.

## Part IV — A change between friends

### 19. The invitation

**Situation:** Nara plans a trip and invites Toren.

```text
Nara: Nor-var I an Varthas.
Toren: Ra var Ya nu?
Nara: Nor-var I an Varthas nor aenor.
Toren: Var I thar-ka.
Nara: Ra var Ya ser I?
Toren: Lia.
```

**English:** I'll go to the city. / When are you going? / I'll go to the city in a moment. / I want to go. / Are you going with me? / Yes.

### 20. Toren changes their mind

**Situation:** Just before leaving, Toren realizes they need rest.

```text
Toren: Em. Rinaen I felkal.
Nara: Ra var Ya ser I?
Toren: Ka. Ver var I an Varthas.
Nara: Ra mbaen I Ya?
Toren: Ka. Ver mbaen Ya I.
Nara: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** Um. I'm tired. / Are you going with me? / No. I'm not going to the city. / Can I help you? / No. Don't help me. / Okay.

### 21. What Toren meant

**Situation:** Toren returns to the conversation after a pause.

```text
Toren: Rakel, Nara.
Nara: Vael aen Ya.
Toren: Tha-rethnemaen Ya I.
Nara: Rakel?
Toren: Rinaen I felkal. Ver var I an Varthas.
Nara: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** Pardon, Nara. / Please speak. / You misunderstood me. / Pardon? / I'm tired. I'm not going to the city. / I understand.

### 22. Water first

**Situation:** Nara checks in without pushing the trip.

```text
Nara: Ra rinaen Ya felkal nethnor?
Toren: Lia.
Nara: Rinaen dren an drenkavvek.
Toren: Rinaen I drorav.
Nara: Tal I dren an Ya.
Toren: Drenaen I dren.
```

**English:** Are you still tired? / Yes. / There is water in the flask. / I'm thirsty. / I give you water. / I drink water.

### 23. The next morning

**Situation:** A little rest has changed the mood.

```text
Nara: Nekvar, Ser.
Toren: Nekvar.
Nara: Ra rinaen Ya felkal nethnor?
Toren: Ka. Ver rinaen I felkal.
Nara: Ra var Ya an evan?
Toren: Lia. Var I ser Ya.
```

**English:** Good morning, friend. / Good morning. / Are you still tired? / No. I'm not tired. / Are you going to the market? / Yes. I'm going with you.

### 24. Together this time

**Situation:** They leave, at Toren's pace.

```text
Nara: Nor-var I an evan.
Toren: Var I sera an evan.
Nara: Ra var Ilin an evan servok?
Toren: Lia.
Nara: Dar I an vorkor.
Toren: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** I'll go to the market. / I'm going to the market too. / Are we going to the market together? / Yes. / I'll wait at the door. / Okay.

## Part V — Getting something done

### 25. The door sticks

**Situation:** At home, Toren cannot open the door properly.

```text
Toren: Sivrethaen I kaxaraen vorkor.
Nara: Ra mbaen I Ya?
Toren: Lia. Va tal Ya karvek.
Nara: Tal I karvek.
Toren: Zhaelpralaen I vorkor.
Nara: Dar I ser Ya.
```

**English:** I can't open the door. / Can I help you? / Yes. Take the toolkit. / I take the toolkit. / I repair the door. / I'll wait with you.

### 26. Look again

**Situation:** The first repair does not work.

```text
Nara: Ra kaxaraen Ya vorkor?
Toren: Ka. Sivrethaen I kaxaraen vorkor.
Nara: Kes I rethpral.
Toren: Rakel?
Nara: Va kaxaraen Ya vorkor wek'aen.
Toren: Sivrethaen I kaxaraen vorkor nethnor.
```

**English:** Are you opening the door? / No. I can't open the door. / I see the mistake. / Pardon? / Open the door again. / I still can't open the door.

### 27. The mistake

**Situation:** Toren realizes which part of the door Nara meant.

```text
Toren: Tha-rethnemaen I Ya.
Nara: Lia'ser.
Toren: Kes I rethpral.
Nara: Ra zhaelpralaen Ilin vorkor servok?
Toren: Lia. Zhaelpralaen Ilin vorkor servok.
```

**English:** I misunderstood you. / Okay. / I see the mistake. / Are we repairing the door together? / Yes. We're repairing the door together.

### 28. One more try

**Situation:** They try the door after working together.

```text
Nara: Va kaxaraen Ya vorkor.
Toren: Kaxaraen I vorkor.
Nara: Ra kaxaraen Ya vorkor?
Toren: Lia.
Nara: Lia'ser.
```

**English:** Open the door. / I open the door. / Are you opening the door? / Yes. / Okay.

### 29. Enough for today

**Situation:** The work is done; Toren is tired.

```text
Toren: Rinaen I felkal.
Nara: Rinaen dren an drenkavvek.
Toren: Rinaen dren worlian?
Nara: Lia. Rinaen dren worlian.
Toren: Drenaen I dren.
Nara: Dar I ser Ya.
```

**English:** I'm tired. / There is water in the flask. / Is there enough water? / Yes. There is enough water. / I drink water. / I'll wait with you.

### 30. At the table

**Situation:** They end the day with food and a working door.

```text
Nara: Rinaen morlak ser dren an pralor.
Toren: Rinaen I emilrav.
Nara: Velaen Ilin morlak.
Toren: Lia.
Nara: Jexhaer, Ser.
Toren: Jexhaer.
```

**English:** There is bread and water on the table. / I'm hungry. / We eat bread. / Yes. / Good night, friend. / Good night.

**Performance:** Leave a generous pause after Toren's `Lia.`; the goodnights come after the meal.

## Audio use

Record each scene as one uninterrupted conversation. Keep individual speaker turns as separate clips too. The voices should react to the situation: a surprised `Rakel?`, a brief `Lia`, a pause before changing a plan. The English meanings are for subtitle and direction work; they are not part of the Celan recording.

# Celan everyday conversations — Volume 1

Thirty short conversations, mostly for two voices. Conversation 2 uses three voices. The **Celan script** under each title is the recording copy. Each English segment follows one speaker turn and translates what that turn actually says. Speaker A, Speaker B, and (where present) Speaker C can be played by any voices. Read the written pronoun **I** as **ee** and the written **x** as **ks**. Leave a natural beat between turns. These are newly composed conversations built around established words, examples, and grammar; they are not quotations from the example bank.

## 1. A morning meeting

**Situation:** Two friends meet on their way out.

```text
A: Nekvar!
B: Nekvar, Ser!
A: Ra var Ya lo?
B: Var I an evan.
A: Var I sera an evan.
B: Ra var Ilin an evan?
A: Lia.
B: Var Ilin an evan.
```

**English:** Good morning! / Good morning, friend! / Where are you going? / I'm going to the market. / I'm going to the market too. / Are we going to the market? / Yes. / We're going to the market.

## 2. Names and introductions

**Situation:** A friend introduces Toren and Nara. Their names are character names for these scripts.

**Speaker direction:** A is the introducer, B is Toren, and C is Nara. Use three distinct voices throughout this exchange.

```text
A: Zhilva!
B: Zhilva!
A: Ra aen Ya kelrin-ya?
B: Aen I kelrin-ian: Toren.
C: Aen I kelrin-ian: Nara.
A: Ra var Ya lo?
B: Var I an evan.
C: Var I sera an evan.
```

**English:** Hello! / Hello! / Will you say your name? / I say my name: Toren. / I say my name: Nara. / Where are you going? / I'm going to the market. / I'm going to the market too.

**Language note:** The `kelrin-ian` line is newly constructed from the attested `kelrin-ya` question and the personal possessive pattern.

## 3. Are you tired?

**Situation:** A friend notices the other has had a long day.

```text
A: Ra rinaen Ya felkal?
B: Lia. Rinaen I felkal.
A: Ra mbaen I Ya?
B: Lia. Va mbaen Ya I.
A: Va var Ya an deni.
B: Dar I an xaror.
A: Rinaen dren an drenkavvek.
B: Lia. Drenaen I drosel.
```

**English:** Are you tired? / Yes. I'm tired. / May I help you? / Yes. Help me. / Come here. / I'll wait in the room. / There's water in the flask. / Yes. I'll drink clean water.

## 4. Breakfast together

**Situation:** Two people decide what to eat and drink.

```text
A: Rinaen I emilrav.
B: Rinaen morlak ser dren.
A: Ra drenaen Ya dren reth phelvin?
B: Drenaen I phelvin.
A: Drenaen I dren.
B: Velaen Ilin morlak.
A: Lia.
```

**English:** I'm hungry. / There's bread and water. / Will you drink water or a comforting drink? / I'll drink a comforting drink. / I'll drink water. / We eat bread. / Yes.

## 5. Leaving home

**Situation:** One person is about to go out; the other checks the plan.

```text
A: Kaxaraen I vorkor.
B: Ra var Ya lo?
A: Nor-var I an Eshvan.
B: Ra rinaen emil an teremil?
A: Ka. Ver rinaen emil an teremil.
B: Dar I an teremil.
A: Zharinor!
B: Zharinor!
```

**English:** I'm opening the door. / Where are you going? / I'll go to the shop. / Is there food at home? / No. There isn't food at home. / I'll wait at home. / See you soon! / See you soon!

## 6. At the market

**Situation:** A customer asks about food and buys bread.

```text
A: Zhilva!
B: Zhilva!
A: Ra rinaen morlak an van?
B: Lia. Rinaen morlak zhaeleth.
A: Anvanesh I morlak.
B: Tal I morlak an Ya.
```

**English:** Hello! / Hello! / Is there bread at the shop? / Yes. There is fresh bread. / I'll buy bread. / I give you bread.

**Recording note:** The exchange deliberately omits a price; it does not teach how to pay yet.

## 7. Going together

**Situation:** Two travelers discover they are going to the same city.

```text
A: Ra var Ya lo?

B: Lia. Var Ilin an Varthas.
```

**English:** Where are you going? / I'm going to the city. / Are you going with a friend? / No. / I'm going to the city too. / Yes. We're going to the city.

## 9. I didn't understand

**Situation:** One speaker needs the other to say something more clearly.

```text
A: Nor-var I an evan.
B: Ver nemaen I.
B: Va selnemaen Ya kel.
A: Var I an evan.
B: Ra var Ya an evan?
A: Lia.
B: Nemaen I.
```

**English:** I'll go to the market. / I don't understand. / Clarify the words. / I'm going to the market. / Are you going to the market? / Yes. / I understand.

## 10. Can I help?

**Situation:** Someone sees a friend carrying things.

```text
A: Ra mbaen I Ya?
B: Lia. Va mbaen Ya I.
A: Ra rinaen kavvek an eshpral?
B: Lia.
A: Tal I kavvek thal eshpral.
B: Va dor Ya kavvek an pralor.
A: Dor I kavvek an pralor.
B: Lia.
```

**English:** May I help you? / Yes. Help me. / Is the jar on the shelf? / Yes. / I take the jar from the shelf. / Put the jar on the table. / I put the jar on the table. / Yes.

**English:** Hello, friend! / Hello! / Enter the room. / I'm hungry. / The food is fresh. / Is there bread? / Yes. There is bread and water. / We eat bread.

## 12. Good night

**English:** I'm tired. / I'm tired too. / I'm closing the window. / I'm closing the door. / We'll sleep in bed. / Yes. / Good night! / Good night!

## 15. Where is the bowl?

**Situation:** A bowl has been moved while a meal is prepared.

```text
A: Ra rinaen drenvor an pralor?
B: Ka. Ver rinaen drenvor an pralor.
A: Ra kes Ya mu?
B: Kes I drenvor an xaror.
A: Tal I drenvor.
B: Va dor Ya drenvor an pralor.
```

**English:** Is the bowl on the table? / No. The bowl isn't on the table. / What do you see? / I see the bowl in the room. / I take the bowl. / Put the bowl on the table.

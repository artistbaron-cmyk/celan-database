# Everyday conversations — Volume 3, English-first draft

Savi keeps the shared supplies, Dalen builds and repairs, and Meva carries goods and messages. These conversations take place over several days and several trips. Each scene can be understood on its own. Actions in italics are not spoken. This is a dialogue draft for review; Celan translation and recording copy come after the English exchanges work.

## 1. One for Meva

*Dalen sets four full water flasks beside Meva's bag. Meva checks the bag while Savi closes the supply cupboard.*

**Dalen:** Four flasks for the village. That's everything.\
**Meva:** And my water?\
**Dalen:** You brought your own flask, didn't you?\
**Meva:** I left it here yesterday.\
**Savi:** It's by the door. Empty.\
**Dalen:** I'll fill it.

*Dalen fills the spare flask and hands it to Meva.*

**Dalen:** Five flasks. Can you carry all of these?\
**Meva:** Yes. Let me drink before I close the bag.\

*Meva drinks, puts the flask in the bag, and closes it.*

**Meva:** All right. I'm leaving now. Did I miss anything?\
**Savi:** I'm still checking the list.

*Meva pauses with the bag in hand.*

## 2. Leave it empty

*The next morning, Savi finds an empty basket among the packed supplies.*

**Savi:** This basket is empty. Did you forget to fill it?\
**Dalen:** Leave it that way. Meva is bringing grain back in it.\
**Savi:** Then why is it beside the food we're sending?\
**Dalen:** I put all her baskets together.\
**Savi:** She may fill it before she leaves.\
**Dalen:** Fair. I'll put it by the return list.

*He moves the basket. Savi marks it on the list.*

## 3. One too many

*Meva lifts the bag, then puts it down again.*

**Meva:** There are six bundles in here. We agreed on five.\
**Dalen:** I added the spare rope.\
**Meva:** I can carry the rope or the extra food. Choose.\
**Savi:** Keep the food. Dalen can send the rope later.\
**Dalen:** I thought the bridge might need it.\
**Meva:** Then I'll ask when I get there. Don't make me carry a guess.

*Dalen takes the rope out. Meva lifts the bag again and keeps it.*

## 4. One for each

*Four workers will stay overnight. Savi sets three blankets on the table.*

**Savi:** Three blankets. That should do.\
**Dalen:** There are four workers.\
**Savi:** One will be on watch.\
**Dalen:** And when their watch ends?\
**Savi:** They'll need one too. Is there another?\
**Dalen:** In the cupboard. I'll get it.

## 5. The other pouch

*Dalen counts the coins on the table. Meva waits for the payment she must carry.*

**Dalen:** Two coins are missing.\
**Savi:** I put them in a separate pouch.\
**Dalen:** Why?\
**Savi:** Those are for the road. The rest are for the grain.\
**Meva:** Give me both pouches. I'll know which is which if you mark them.\
**Dalen:** I'll mark them. I don't want you paying for grain with the road money.

*Savi slides the second pouch across; Dalen marks it.*

## 6. Water before the road

*Meva fills her flask from the water Savi has set aside.*

**Savi:** Leave some water for the people here.\
**Meva:** I am.\
**Savi:** That little bit won't last them until evening.\
**Meva:** Then tell me what they need. I can't judge from the jug.\
**Savi:** Two full flasks. I'll fill them first.\
**Meva:** Good. I'll take what's left.

## 7. Half the water

*Dalen sets a small water container beside Savi and puts a larger one with Meva's supplies.*

**Savi:** That's all you're leaving here?\
**Dalen:** I left you one.\
**Savi:** The other one holds much more.\
**Dalen:** You asked for half.\
**Savi:** Half the water.

*Dalen takes the larger container back.*

**Dalen:** I'll measure it.\
**Meva:** Please do. I need to leave soon.\
**Savi:** Give us a moment.

## 8. Keep a quarter

*Savi measures the grain and ties off one sack. Meva reaches for it; Savi stops her.*

**Meva:** This one isn't coming?\
**Savi:** I'm keeping a quarter of the grain here for your return.\
**Meva:** I won't need grain on the return trip.\
**Savi:** The people here will. We'll have used the rest by then.\
**Meva:** All right. Put it away before Dalen adds it to my bag.\
**Dalen:** I heard that.

*Savi carries the sack back to the cupboard.*

## 9. Five of eight

*Savi has measured eight portions of grain. Meva checks what has been loaded.*

**Meva:** Your list says five-eighths of the grain goes to the village. I see four measures.\
**Dalen:** The fifth is still on the scale.\
**Meva:** It needs to be in the bag, not on the scale.\
**Dalen:** I was checking the weight.\
**Savi:** Finish checking. Meva can leave when all five are here.\
**Meva:** Thank you. I don't want to come back for one measure.

## 10. Enough for whom?

*Dalen closes a food basket. Savi keeps looking at the remaining bread.*

**Dalen:** That's enough bread for everyone here.\
**Savi:** You mean everyone here now.\
**Dalen:** Who else is coming?\
**Savi:** The workers return before evening.\
**Dalen:** I thought they were eating at the village.\
**Savi:** They were. The work ended early.\
**Dalen:** Then I'll leave this basket open.

## 11. The wrong length

*Dalen holds a cut rope against the space where he needs it. It falls short.*

**Dalen:** This rope won't reach.\
**Savi:** I cut six handspans, like you said.\
**Dalen:** I needed six strides.\
**Savi:** You said handspans.\
**Dalen:** You're right. I gave you the wrong unit.\
**Savi:** There's a longer piece. Measure it with me this time.

## 12. A stride apart

*Meva places a marker on the ground. Dalen checks its distance from the first marker.*

**Meva:** Here?\
**Dalen:** One more stride.\
*Meva moves it toward Dalen.*\
**Dalen:** From the first marker, not from me.\
**Meva:** You moved while I was walking.\
**Dalen:** I did. Put it down there; I'll come to you.\
**Meva:** Good. Stay by the first one this time.

## 13. The other five

*Meva reads a tag marked “five” and picks up the coin pouch. Savi stops her.*

**Savi:** The grain, Meva. Five measures of grain.\
**Meva:** Dalen tied the tag to the coins.\
**Dalen:** I was marking the price.\
**Savi:** Then mark the pouch too.\
**Meva:** Please. I nearly took the wrong five.\
**Dalen:** I'll write the unit on both.

## 14. Too heavy

*Meva lifts a crate and puts it back down.*

**Meva:** I can lift it. I can't carry it that far.\
**Dalen:** The tools have to go today.\
**Savi:** Which ones will they use first?\
**Dalen:** The small set. The rest can wait.\
**Meva:** Put that set in my bag. I can take the crate tomorrow.\
**Dalen:** Tomorrow, then. I won't add anything else to it.

## 15. From the door

*Savi marks a place for a shelf. Dalen stops with the shelf in his hands.*

**Dalen:** I meant three handspans from the door.\
**Savi:** I started at the table.\
**Dalen:** I should've said where to start.\
**Savi:** Put the shelf down. We'll measure from the door together.\
**Dalen:** Before I make holes in the wall.\
**Savi:** That's why I stopped you.

## 16. The clamp by Savi

*Savi holds up one end of the shelf. The other end starts to slip.*

**Savi:** Dalen, the clamp here by my foot.\
**Dalen:** I see a hammer.\
**Savi:** Under it. Quickly.\
*Dalen moves the hammer and finds the clamp.*\
**Dalen:** Got it. Keep holding the shelf.\
**Savi:** I'm trying.\
*Dalen fastens the clamp; Savi lets go.*

## 17. The box by Meva

*Savi carries a folded cloth across her arms. Meva stands beside two boxes.*

**Savi:** Could you put that small box by you on the table?\
**Meva:** This one?\
**Savi:** The other. The one without a lid.\
**Meva:** This is the one I was about to carry away.\
**Savi:** I need it for the cloth first.\
**Meva:** Then I'll wait. Set the cloth down here.

## 18. The one beyond them

*Dalen and Savi search the workroom for a long piece of wood. Meva is at the doorway.*

**Dalen:** None of these boards is long enough.\
**Meva:** There's another piece beyond the table.\
**Savi:** The one near you?\
**Meva:** Farther back, against the wall.\
*Savi goes to it and lifts one end.*\
**Savi:** This one?\
**Dalen:** That's the length I need. Meva, can you take the other end?\
**Meva:** Yes. Savi, lift when I do.

## 19. Whose left?

*Dalen carries a crate while Meva, facing him, guides him around a table.*

**Meva:** Move the crate left.\
**Dalen:** It's against the wall.\
**Meva:** My left. Sorry.\
**Dalen:** You're facing me.\
**Meva:** All right. Toward the door.\
**Dalen:** That I can do.\
*He clears the table and puts the crate down.*

## 20. Outside the door

*Savi looks for a dry sack as rain begins.*

**Savi:** Where did you leave the empty sack?\
**Dalen:** Outside the door, under the roof.\
**Meva:** The roof leaks there.\
**Dalen:** I didn't know that.\
**Savi:** Bring it inside before the rain reaches it.\
*Dalen fetches the sack.*\
**Dalen:** Still dry. Just.

## 21. Behind the load

*Meva reaches into a crowded storeroom for a lantern.*

**Meva:** Where's the lantern? It was here this morning.\
**Dalen:** Behind the tall crate. I moved it while I was sweeping.\
**Meva:** I can't reach it with the crate there.\
**Dalen:** Hold the door. I'll move the crate.\
*He shifts it; Meva takes the lantern.*\
**Meva:** Next time, tell me before you hide the thing I need at dusk.\
**Dalen:** I didn't think you'd be back this late.

## 22. Between the sacks

*Two sacks stand near the doorway. Savi has misplaced a small tool bag.*

**Savi:** Did you take the tool bag?\
**Meva:** No. I saw it between the sacks when I came in.\
*Savi looks and finds it.*\
**Savi:** There it is. I thought it was inside the left one.\
**Meva:** I wouldn't put tools in a grain sack.\
**Savi:** Dalen might.\
**Dalen:** I can hear you both.

## 23. The closed gate

*Meva returns to the workshop sooner than expected.*

**Savi:** You're back already?\
**Meva:** The gate on the short path is closed.\
**Dalen:** Can you go through the old passage?\
**Meva:** Not with this load. It's too narrow.\
**Dalen:** Then take the open road across the field.\
**Meva:** I will. First I'm putting this down for a moment.

## 24. Leave it outside

*Meva comes to the door with mud on her boots and a folded message in her hand.*

**Savi:** Leave the boots outside. I just cleaned the floor.\
**Meva:** The message can't wait.\
**Savi:** Hand it to me from there.\
*Meva passes it across the threshold.*\
**Meva:** See? I didn't bring the mud in.\
**Savi:** You can come in when you take the boots off.\
**Meva:** Then I'll sit on the step while you read.

## 25. The nearer stop

*Meva has two deliveries and is deciding which place to visit first.*

**Meva:** I'm going to the village before the market.\
**Dalen:** The market is nearer from here.\
**Meva:** The village needs its tools before noon.\
**Dalen:** I'll put the village bag on top.\
**Meva:** Thanks. I can stop at the market on my way back.\
*Dalen moves the bag where Meva can reach it first.*

## 26. How far from here?

*Dalen considers carrying a repaired lamp to a nearby village himself.*

**Dalen:** How far is the village from here?\
**Meva:** Near. You can see its roofs from the hill.\
**Dalen:** Then I can take the lamp this afternoon.\
**Meva:** You can. Ask for Savi's directions before you go.\
**Dalen:** I know the way.\
**Meva:** That's what you said before you turned left at the market.

## 27. Near, but a long walk

*Dalen comes back for water after looking toward the village across the river.*

**Dalen:** It looks close. Why did Meva tell me to leave early?\
**Savi:** The bridge is closed. You'll have to walk around the river.\
**Dalen:** I can see the village from here.\
**Savi:** You can see it. You can't cross here.\
*She offers him a flask.*\
**Dalen:** All right. Give me the water.\
*Savi hands it over. Dalen starts toward the longer route.*

## 28. At least five

*Savi checks the water going with Meva.*

**Savi:** They asked for at least five full flasks. You've packed four.\
**Meva:** The fifth one leaks.\
**Dalen:** I can replace its stopper.\
**Meva:** Can you do it before I leave?\
**Dalen:** Let me try it now. If it still leaks, I'll bring a different flask.\
**Savi:** I won't mark five on the list until it holds water.\
*Dalen changes the stopper and turns the filled flask over. It stays dry.*\
**Meva:** Good. I'll close the bag.

## 29. No more than two

*Savi brings three crates to the doorway. Meva has room for two.*

**Meva:** No more than two crates today.\
**Savi:** The third has to reach them before evening.\
**Dalen:** I can carry it if I go with Meva.\
**Meva:** Are you coming all the way this time?\
**Dalen:** I am. I need to check the lamp I repaired.\
**Savi:** Good. Two for Meva, one for Dalen. I'll mark it that way.

## 30. Back with the flask

*Meva returns at dusk and sets an empty flask on the table. Dalen comes in behind her.*

**Savi:** You brought your flask back.\
**Meva:** Empty. The other four stayed at the village.\
**Dalen:** And the crates made it there. All three.\
**Savi:** Good. I was about to count them again.\
**Meva:** Tomorrow. Let me sit down first.\
**Savi:** I was going to say the same thing.\
*Meva sits; Dalen pulls out a chair for Savi.*

# Dialogue pilot 01 — A morning meeting

Source: [Everyday conversations, Volume 1](everyday_conversations_volume_01.md), conversation 1.

Use ElevenLabs **Dialogue mode**, model `eleven_v4`. Generate the eight turns as one conversation. Speaker A uses **Ayinde - young British Nigerian** (`77aEIu0qStu8Jwv1EdhX`); Speaker B uses **Lily - Velvety Actress** (`pFZP5JQG7iQjIQuC4Bku`). Keep a natural beat between turns. The source text is unchanged; only standalone written `I` becomes `-ee` in the spoken input, following the Celan audio pronunciation convention.

| Turn | Speaker | Spoken input |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A · Ayinde | Nekvar! |
| 2 | B · Lily | Nekvar, Ser! |
| 3 | A · Ayinde | Ra var Ya lo? |
| 4 | B · Lily | Var -ee an evan. |
| 5 | A · Ayinde | Var -ee sera an evan. |
| 6 | B · Lily | Ra var Ilin an evan? |
| 7 | A · Ayinde | Lia. |
| 8 | B · Lily | Var Ilin an evan. |

Review the first complete take for speaker consistency, the standalone `I` sound, pacing, and whether `Ilin` remains intact.
